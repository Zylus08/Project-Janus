# PRIMARY MISSION

Investigate whether Janus Classical X19's substantial search-tree reduction reflects genuinely improved alpha-beta move ordering, and determine why its competitive results apparently deteriorate at longer time controls.

Preserve the useful aspects of X19's staged capture ordering while preventing tactical omissions, unsafe capture deferral, inaccurate SEE classifications and unreliable history-score prioritization.

The milestone has five central objectives:

1. Prove that the restructured MovePicker preserves complete and legal move enumeration.
2. Identify whether deferring unfavorable captures damages search correctness or practical playing strength.
3. Determine whether X19's apparent slower-time regression is reproducible and attributable to capture ordering.
4. Investigate history-score reliability only after isolating capture-ordering behavior.
5. Produce a stronger engine candidate only if independent evidence supports promotion.

# PHASE 1 — COMPLETE MOVEPICKER CORRECTNESS AUDIT

This is the highest-priority correctness phase.

Inspect the actual source code for X7, X12 and X19.

Reconstruct the exact move-selection state machine.

The P1.14 report describes five stages:

1. Favorable captures.
2. Killer moves.
3. Countermoves.
4. History-ranked quiet moves.
5. Unfavorable captures.

However, the report does not explicitly describe the treatment of the principal variation or transposition-table move.

Establish where TT/PV moves are handled and whether they can be duplicated or omitted.

## Exhaustive move-set preservation

For every tested position:

1. Generate the complete legal move set using an independent reference enumerator.
2. Generate the complete move sequence yielded by MovePicker.
3. Normalize move encodings.
4. Compare the resulting sets.
5. Detect duplicates.
6. Detect omissions.
7. Validate every emitted move.
8. Verify stage membership.

Test both single-thread and multithreaded configurations.

### Required position classes

- Starting position.
- Open tactical middlegames.
- Closed positions.
- King in check.
- Double check.
- Pinned pieces.
- Castling.
- En passant.
- Quiet promotions.
- Capture promotions.
- Underpromotions.
- Exchange sacrifices.
- Discovered attacks.
- Forced recaptures.
- Stalemate-adjacent positions.
- Checkmate-in-one positions.
- Forced defensive captures.
- Pure pawn endgames.
- Queen and rook endgames.

## Stage invariants

Every legal move must be yielded exactly once.

A TT move already returned must never reappear in another stage.

Killer and countermove entries must be validated for legality.

Capture and quiet classifications must handle promotions and en passant correctly.

If the side to move is in check, verify that the special evasion path is complete and does not blindly apply normal ordering assumptions.

### Release gate
