## 1. Dataset Provenance & Architectural Principles

The training, validation, and testing corpora for **Project Janus NNUE** are derived exclusively from verified internal match tournaments generated during Phase 1:
- `match1_r1_vs_x22s_fast.pgn` (Milestone P1.22)
- `match3_r1_vs_x7_fast.pgn` (Milestone P1.22)
- `match5_r1_4t_vs_x12_fast.pgn` (Milestone P1.22)
- `match1_x26s_vs_x22s_fast.pgn` (Milestone P1.21)
- `match3_x26s_vs_x7_fast.pgn` (Milestone P1.21)
- `match_1_x24-s_vs_x22-s_ref_1t.pgn` (Milestone P1.19)

### Zero External Pretrained Network Contamination:
No external pretrained evaluation nets (e.g. Stockfish `nn-*.nnue`) or proprietary third-party commercial datasets were utilized. All target evaluations represent authentic competitive game trajectories generated by Janus Classical engines.

## 2. Leakage Prevention Protocol

1. **Game-Level Partitioning:**
   The 583 ingested games were partitioned randomly with a fixed deterministic seed (`seed=42`) into:
   - **Training Set:** 466 games (80.0%)
   - **Validation Set:** 58 games (10.0%)
   - **Test Set:** 59 games (10.0%)
   Positions were extracted *only after* game partition assignment, guaranteeing that positions from the same game or opening line cannot appear across different splits.
2. **Global Position Deduplication:**
   A global set of canonical FEN strings (`board.fen()`) was maintained across all extraction steps. Any position previously encountered in another game was rejected.
3. **Opening & Deep Endgame Truncation:**
   Initial book plies ($1\text{--}7$) were excluded to avoid memorizing symmetric starting moves. Extreme endgame repetitions ($>120$ plies) were trimmed to avoid biased zero-sum end states.
4. **Target Perspective Normalization:**
   All ground-truth labels are formulated from the **side-to-move** perspective:
   $$z_{\text{target}} = \begin{cases} z_{\text{white}} & \text{if White to move} \\ 1.0 - z_{\text{white}} & \text{if Black to move} \end{cases}$$
   where $z_{\text{white}} \in \{1.0\text{ (Win)}, 0.5\text{ (Draw)}, 0.0\text{ (Loss)}\}$.
