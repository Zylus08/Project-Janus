### 3. Formal Gate Decision: H3 Training Properly Deferred to Milestone P2.3

**DECISION: DO NOT TRAIN A PREMATURE H3 CANDIDATE IN MILESTONE P2.2.**

**Scientific Justification:**
1. Training another candidate model on the existing 40,736-position dataset using the flawed DP-PS-768 architecture would merely produce another weak model that cannot understand king safety or pawn shelter.
2. Training the expanded HalfKP-40960 architecture on only 40,736 positions would severely overfit, as $5.25\text{M}$ parameters cannot be resolved from $40\text{k}$ examples.
3. Premature training violates Non-Negotiable Rule 6 (*"Do not train a larger model until the original model is understood"*) and Rule 13 (*"Do not create H3 unless the evidence supports it"*).

**Designation for Milestone P2.3:**
Candidate **Janus Hybrid H3** is formally scheduled for Milestone P2.3 as the **Full-Scale HalfKP-40960 Neural Evaluation Architecture**, trained on $\ge 500,000$ self-play positions generated by the verified Phase 10 pipeline with integrated search accumulator stacks.
