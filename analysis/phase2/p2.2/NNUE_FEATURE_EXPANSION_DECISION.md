### 1. Executive Summary & Problem Formulation

Milestone P2.2 forensic investigations (Phases 1, 4, and 8) have proven that the existing **DP-PS-768** input representation cannot learn positional chess.
Because DP-PS-768 contains no king-square conditioning in its feature formulas:
$$\text{Feature}_{\text{White}} = (\text{Color} \times 6 + \text{PieceType}) \times 64 + \text{Square}$$
every piece contributes independently to the accumulator. The network cannot represent:
- Pawns sheltering the friendly king.
- Enemy knights/bishops targeting squares around the friendly king.
- Open files bearing down on the castled king position.

All non-linear interactions are forced into the tiny 32-neuron hidden layer, which cannot capture complex positional themes without king-conditioned sparse feature cross-terms.

### 3. Deep Architectural Analysis

#### 3.1 Why F1 (Standard HalfKP-40960) is the Industry Standard
In HalfKP-40960, every piece on the board is indexed jointly with the friendly king square:
$$\text{Feature} = \text{KingSq} \times (10 \times 64) + \text{PieceType} \times 64 + \text{PieceSq}$$
- When a white pawn sits on `f2` and the white king is on `g1`, this activates a unique feature index encoding: *"Pawn on f2 defending King on g1"*.
- If the king moves to `e1`, the active feature index changes to: *"Pawn on f2 with King on e1"*.
- This allows the linear accumulator to directly learn specialized weights for every possible king shield, exposed file, and attacking diagonal without requiring deep hidden layers.

#### 3.2 The King-Move Refresh Trade-off
- On normal moves ($95\%$ of all plies), HalfKP updates in exact $O(1)$ time (2 additions, 2 subtractions).
- On friendly king moves ($5\%$ of plies), the friendly perspective accumulator cannot be updated incrementally; it requires a full refresh ($N \approx 16$ additions of 128 elements).
- Because king moves occur on only 1 out of every 20 moves, the amortized cost of king refreshes increases leaf evaluation time by less than $6\%$.

#### 3.3 The Data Volume Constraint
With $5.25\text{M}$ parameters, training HalfKP-40960 on only 40k positions (the P2.1 dataset) would result in extreme overfitting and sparse feature starvation (most of the 40,960 features would never be observed). 
HalfKP-40960 strictly requires a dataset of **at least 500,000 to 1,000,000 positions** generated by the verified Phase 10 self-play pipeline.

---

### 4. Strategic Feature Decision

1. **Rejection of Premature F1 Training in P2.2:**
   Do not train a 40,960-dimensional network during Milestone P2.2 on the 40k P2.1 dataset.
2. **Adoption of HalfKP-40960 for Milestone P2.3:**
   Formally select **F1 (Standard HalfKP-40960)** as the target architecture for Milestone P2.3, paired with the 1,000,000-position self-play generation campaign established in Phase 10.
3. **Preservation of F0:**
   Candidate H1 (F0 DP-PS-768) remains the frozen reference baseline for Phase 2 infrastructure.
