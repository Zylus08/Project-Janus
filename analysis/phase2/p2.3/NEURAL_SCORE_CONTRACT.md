### 1. Canonical Training Perspective

To prevent perspective mismatch and sign contamination across training, search, and UCI communication, Milestone P2.3 establishes a single canonical perspective:

$$\textbf{Canonical Convention: Side-To-Move-Relative Centipawns (STM-cp)}$$

Under this convention, a positive score always indicates that the side whose turn it is to move holds an advantage.


### 2. Mathematical Score-Scale Transformations

The score conversion utilizes a standard logistic sigmoid mapping parameterized by temperature $\tau = 400.0\text{ cp}$:

$$p(s) = \sigma\left(\frac{s}{\tau}\right) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-s / \tau}}$$

$$s(p) = \tau \cdot \text{logit}(p) = \tau \cdot \ln\left(\frac{p}{1 - p}\right)$$

#### Invariant Scale Anchor Points:
- $\mathbf{s = 0\text{ cp}} \implies p = 0.500000$ (dead drawn / completely equal).
- $\mathbf{s = +100\text{ cp}} \implies p = 0.562177$ (one pawn advantage).
- $\mathbf{s = +300\text{ cp}} \implies p = 0.679179$ (minor piece advantage).
- $\mathbf{s = +400\text{ cp}} \implies p = 0.731059$ ($1.0$ logit unit).
- $\mathbf{s = +900\text{ cp}} \implies p = 0.904651$ (queen advantage).
- $\mathbf{s = \pm 2000\text{ cp}} \implies p \in [0.006693, 0.993307]$ (clamping threshold).

#### Mate-Score & Outlier Handling:
- Forced checkmate scores ($\pm 30,000\text{ cp}$) generated by the search are outside the continuous evaluation manifold.
- Positions annotated with mate scores or decisive terminal evaluations are clamped to $\pm 2,000\text{ cp}$ ($\pm 5.0$ logits) during dataset curation.
- This prevents gradient explosion while preserving strong monotonic training signals.

### 3. Diagnostic Invariant Specifications

The neural evaluation function $E(\text{Board})$ must strictly satisfy the following operational invariants:

1. **Color Skew-Symmetry:**
   Let $B$ be a position with White to move, and $B'$ be the mirrored position with inverted colors and Black to move.
   $$E(B) = E(B')$$
2. **Tempo & Initiative Realistic Bounds:**
   In the initial starting position (`startpos`), the score is not constrained to be strictly $0\text{ cp}$; first-move initiative may yield a small positive evaluation:
   $$-10\text{ cp} \le E(\text{startpos}) \le +35\text{ cp}$$
3. **Monotonic Material Sensitivity:**
   In quiet, tactically settled positions without counterplay:
   $$E(\text{Position} + Q) > E(\text{Position} + R) > E(\text{Position} + B) \approx E(\text{Position} + N) > E(\text{Position} + P)$$
4. **Side-to-Move Reversal:**
   If the side to move is flipped without altering piece placement on a board with material imbalance:
   $$E(B_{\text{btm}}) \approx - E(B_{\text{wtm}})$$
