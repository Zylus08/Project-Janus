## 5. Architectural Drivers of the Strength Gain

Why did N2 outperform K0 by such a massive margin (+478 Elo)?

1. **Depth Advantage Under Clocks:**
   In fixed-time play with ~75ms per move, Python K0 typically searches to Depth 2 or Depth 3 (averaging 5,000 to 20,000 NPS). Native N2 searches to Depth 4, Depth 5, or Depth 6 (averaging 350,000 to 600,000 NPS).
2. **Tactical Horizon Horizon Effect:**
   At Depth 2-3, K0 frequently enters tactical exchanges without seeing 3-ply and 4-ply refutations (e.g. pinned pieces, removal of the guard, back-rank combinations). N2 routinely sees these refutations and capitalizes on K0's tactical errors.
3. **TT Warm-up & Move Continuity:**
   N2's 1,048,576-entry native transposition table persists between iterative deepening iterations and moves, allowing rapid PV re-searches in sub-millisecond time.
4. **Zero Overhead UCI Loop:**
   Unbuffered native I/O ensures moves are output within microseconds of the time-management cutoff, maximizing the proportion of allotted time dedicated to tree search.
