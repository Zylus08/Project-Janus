## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone P1.0 inaugurates the **Project Janus Classical Engine Supremacy Program** — a long-term, strictly non-machine-learning engineering initiative whose ultimate objective is to develop a native, pure C++17 classical chess engine capable of demonstrating superior playing strength against elite classical benchmarks and systematically challenging unrestricted modern engines.

In Milestone P1.0, the native classical baseline was successfully recovered from the v0.57 freeze anchor (`2b24d13`) in an isolated Git worktree (`project-janus-phase1`). The developmental engine lineage **Janus Classical X** was established, built, and rigorously audited. Comprehensive search profiling, correctness qualification, historical baseline reproduction, and comparative diagnostic benchmarking against official Stockfish 19 were executed.

The empirical results reveal two fundamental bottlenecks:
1. **The Search Efficiency Crisis:** Janus Classical C1 exhibits an Effective Branching Factor of $EBF = 6.557$, searching **20.87 times more nodes** than Stockfish 19 to achieve only depth 7 (seldepth 7) compared to Stockfish's depth 12 (seldepth 17). Furthermore, quiescence search consumes **74.8% of all search nodes** due to unpruned captures, and move generation consumes **84.3% of total CPU time** due to un-staged move generation.
2. **The Classical Evaluation Gap:** C1 relies on only 2 basic evaluation terms (Material + PST), lacking 12 standard classical evaluation families (mobility, pawn structure, king safety, bishop pair, outposts, endgame proximity). This produces a mean evaluation error of **132.48 cp** against Stockfish 19, dropping move agreement to **0.0% in closed pawn structures** and **10.0% in endgames**.

Based on these findings, an 8-stage architectural development roadmap (P1.1 through P1.7) has been ratified, with **Milestone P1.1 (Modern Classical Search)** selected as the immediate priority to collapse the search tree before evaluation enrichment.

### Critical Bottleneck Identification:
- **Move Generation Overhead (84.3% CPU time):** C1 generates all pseudo-legal moves and runs `is_square_attacked()` on the king for every candidate move. Because 78% of cutoffs occur on move 1, checking legality for the remaining 30 moves is wasted computation.
- **Quiescence Search Bloat (74.8% of nodes):** C1 searches every capture in quiescence without checking whether the capture loses material (`UseSEEInQuiescence=false`).

### P1.1 Immediate Prioritization Rationale:
Search tree modernization (NMP, RFP, Aspiration Windows, Quiescence SEE) must precede evaluation enrichment. Introducing advanced evaluation first into an unpruned tree ($EBF = 6.56$) would cause search depth to drop from depth 7 to depth 5 in tournament play, destroying tactical stability. Collapsing the tree via P1.1 creates the deep search foundation required to support enriched evaluation.
