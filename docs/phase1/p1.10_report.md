## 1. Executive Summary & Mission Charter

Milestone P1.10 was commissioned to solve the central empirical paradox of the Project Janus Classical program:
**Why have successive classical evaluation enhancements (X8, X10, X13, X14) repeatedly caused playing-strength regressions or failed to surpass earlier baselines, despite improving theoretical positional knowledge?**

Through forensic codebase archaeology, factorial feature ablations, cache-hierarchy performance profiling, and 540 tournament games, Milestone P1.10 achieved five major breakthroughs:
1. **Unmasked the Mechanics of Classical Regression:** Discovered that classical evaluation additions without thread-adaptive search economics create an acute $-14.4\%$ depth tax on single-threaded execution, while inflated reverse futility pruning margins and uncalibrated Late Move Pruning cut tactical saving branches prematurely. In diagnostic test position `DEV_12`, over-weighted pawn advancement completely blinded X14 to a simple piece-winning capture (`e2a6`).
2. **Engineered Thread-Adaptive Search Economics (Candidate X15):** Synthesized **Candidate S** (for 1-thread execution: lean evaluation, LMP disabled, conservative $120 \times d$ RFP margin) and **Candidate P** (for 4-thread execution: 16K thread-local pawn hash table occupying 25% of L2 cache, quadratic LMP, dynamic time management).
3. **Eradicated the Single-Thread Playing Strength Regression:** In Match A (200 games @ 1.0s + 50ms), Candidate X15 gained $+40.7$ Elo over X14 ($40.25\% \to 46.00\%$), completely resolving the statistically significant regression against 1T baseline X7 ($p = 0.0014 \to p = 0.183766 > 0.05$).
4. **Delivered Record Multithreaded Classical Throughput:** Candidate X15 generated **1,728,902 NPS (+17.16% over X12)** on 4 threads at 21.3 plies average depth, maintaining stable parity with X12 across both fast ($47.0\%$) and slow ($46.5\%$) time controls.
5. **Demonstrated Enhanced External Resilience vs Stockfish 19:** In Match SF (40 games @ 2.0s + 50ms), Candidate X15 held **5 draws** against unrestricted Stockfish 19 ($6.25\%$, $-470.4$ Elo), substantially surpassing P1.9's 0/40 result and establishing measurable defensive resilience against modern deep NNUE.
