## 1. Executive Summary & Core Mission

Milestone P1.12 marks the decisive reboot and scientific stabilization of Project Janus. Across Milestones P1.8 through P1.11, the project experienced recurring cycles of candidate introduction, apparent tactical improvement, and subsequent competitive collapse in head-to-head tournament play, culminating in the severe **$-117.2\text{ Elo}$ regression of Janus Classical X16** in Milestone P1.11.

Milestone P1.12 was chartered to reset engine development to the strongest empirically qualified foundations, identify the true causal mechanisms behind prior failures, establish a rigorous testing standard, and competitively calibrate classical Janus against both historical and modern computer chess ceilings.

### Milestone Achievements & Key Findings:
1. **P1.11 Reporting Reconciliation:**  
   Audited all 5 PGN archives from Milestone P1.11, resolving the textual discrepancy: the dataset contained exactly **580 games** (zero games omitted; "540" was a clerical carryover from P1.10). Empirically refuted the claim that X16 improved resilience against Stockfish 19 (X16 scored only 1 draw on 4T, $-759.1\text{ Elo}$, versus X15's 5 draws on 4T, $-470.4\text{ Elo}$).
2. **LMR Counterfactual Revalidation:**  
   Audited all 39 disputed moves from P1.11 against Stockfish 19 depth 14 ground truth. Proved that **$38 / 39$ ($97.44\%$)** were benign positional alternatives ($\Delta \le 15\text{ cp}$), with only $1 / 39$ ($2.56\%$) representing an actual tactical blunder (`END_04`, pawn breakthrough). P1.11's claim of a "$33.3\%$ tactical error rate" was a logical fallacy that conflated move disagreement with tactical error.
3. **Causal Feature Decomposition (`F0`--`F5`):**  
   Isolated each component of P1.11's intervention on clean baselines. Proved that unconditional static eval calls (F1) and RFP margin expansion (F2) had negligible impacts ($-3.5\%$ and $-0.03\%$ NPS). The fatal trigger was **`!improving` LMR reduction dampening**, which caused an exponential explosion in Effective Branching Factor ($3.762 \to 4.255$) and a catastrophic **$-2.19$ ply collapse in search depth**, blundering basic endgame opposition (`END_01`, `END_03`).
4. **Three-Tier Competitive Reference Ladder:**  
   Formalized the hierarchy: Tier 1 (Frozen Janus X7/X12 controls), Tier 2 (Stockfish 11, historical classical non-NNUE SOTA), Tier 3 (Stockfish 19, modern unrestricted NNUE SOTA). Stockfish 11 was built, certified, and deployed (`E:\ChessXML\tools\stockfish_11\stockfish_11.exe`).
5. **JCTS v2 Testing Standard:**  
   Adopted the Janus Classical Tournament Standard v2, mandating SPRT gating, paired openings, standardized adjudication thresholds ($\pm 600\text{ cp}$ resignation, $\pm 15\text{ cp}$ draw), and mandatory slower-TC replication.
6. **Janus Classical X17 Implementation & Freeze:**  
   Implemented Candidate X17, branching from clean X7 (1T) and X12 (4T) foundations with a single, targeted, zero-overhead safeguard: exempting quiet pawn moves from LMR in pure pawn endgames (`non_pawn_mat == 0`). Passed $100\%$ of Stage 1 correctness benchmarks (Perft 1-5, Symmetry, Mate in 1-3, Determinism, Low-Clock Resilience).
7. **Tournament Strength & Calibration (410 Games Played):**  
   - Internal Matches: X17-S scored **$51.5\%$ ($+10.4\text{ Elo}$)** fast and **$50.0\%$ ($0.0\text{ Elo}$)** slow against X7 (1T); X17-P scored **$50.0\%$ ($0.0\text{ Elo}$)** against X12 (4T). Fully recovered from X16's $-117\text{ Elo}$ drop to statistical parity.
   - Tier 2 Calibration: Stockfish 11 swept Janus 80-0, establishing a classical headroom ceiling of $\sim 500\text{--}550\text{ Elo}$.
   - Tier 3 Calibration: X17-S held **4 draws ($5.0\%$)** against unrestricted Stockfish 19 at 1T.
8. **Final Research Decision — Category D (Baseline Reboot):**  
   Janus Classical X7 (1T) and Janus Classical X12 (4T) are formally re-certified as authoritative production baselines. Intermediate branches X8 through X16 are permanently retired. Candidate X17 is frozen as an archived research reference.
