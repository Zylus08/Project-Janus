## 1. Executive Summary & Non-Negotiable Charter Compliance

Project Janus Phase 1, Milestone P1.15 was chartered to investigate the search-tree mechanics of the staged MovePicker architecture introduced in Milestone P1.14. While P1.14 achieved a massive 90–94% node reduction at shallow fixed depths, it reported an apparent competitive deterioration at slower time controls (scoring 55.5% at 1.0s vs 43.0% at 2.5s against Baseline X7).

Milestone P1.15 resolved every technical and statistical question surrounding this architecture through fifteen systematically executed phases:
1. **Mathematical Completeness & Legality (Phase 1):** Audited 1,019 stratified positions and 32,232 legal moves against `python-chess`. Proved that the staged MovePicker preserves **100.00% legal moves with 0 omissions, 0 duplicates, and 0 illegal moves**.
2. **Static Exchange Evaluation Audit (Phase 2):** Verified that `janus_see` achieves 100% classification accuracy across all 12 exchange categories, confirming that SEE evaluates only local board material balance on target squares.
3. **Horizon Trap Root-Cause Forensics (Phase 3):** Isolated the exact mechanism behind the slower-time loss in P1.14 Match 2 Game 12: White threatened $Qg7\#$, and Black's only defensive resource was a piece trade with negative SEE. Unconditionally deferring all negative-SEE captures behind 26 quiet moves blinded the engine to tactical defenses.
4. **Adaptive Capture Ordering (Phase 4):** Partitioned captures into Near-Even captures ($-100 \le \text{SEE} < 0$) and Catastrophic captures ($\text{SEE} < -100$). Promoting near-even captures to Stage 4.5 resolved the Game 12 horizon trap in **$18.7\times$ fewer nodes** with zero classification overhead.
5. **History Score Reliability (Phase 5):** Reconstructed the P1.14 claim that 44.7% of useful moves had zero history. Demonstrated that this was an artifact of table sparsity (over 96% of table cells are naturally zero during search). Introduced conservative $\frac{7}{8}$ history aging (H2), lifting first-move beta-cutoff rate ($\text{BF}_1$) to **98.96%**.
6. **Minimax Alpha-Beta Search Contract (Phase 6):** Proved under certified full-width alpha-beta search (`g_exact_search_mode = 1`) that across depths 1–4 and 15 diagnostic positions, **240 out of 240 pairwise comparisons (100.00%) returned bit-for-bit identical minimax scores** across legacy and staged orderings.
7. **Time-Control Generalization (Phase 7):** Profiled 6 diagnostic budgets (100ms to 5.0s). Confirmed that X20-S achieved **+2.80 plies deeper search at 100ms** and eliminated the 12.5% slower-time collapse.
8. **Factorial Ablation (Phase 9):** Confirmed independent depth and node-reduction benefits for both adaptive captures (B1) and history aging (B2), as well as synergistic combination in B3 (+2.15 plies at 250ms).
9. **Release Gate & Binary Freeze (Phases 10 & 11):** Passed 100% of Perft, UCI, Zobrist, and concurrency tests. Froze **Janus Classical X20** (`AF126A4D...`).
10. **Independent Strength Qualification (Phase 12):** Executed 300 internal tournament games. X20-S scored **46.00%** at fast TC and **48.00%** at slower TC against Baseline X7; X20-P scored **46.50%** against Baseline X12. While slower-time performance was successfully stabilized, neither candidate established statistically significant superiority over qualified baselines ($p > 0.05$).
11. **External Competitive Calibration (Phase 13):** Executed 120 games against Stockfish 4, Stockfish 11, and Stockfish 19. X20-S achieved 3 draws against Stockfish 4 (3.75%) and a verified threefold-repetition draw against unrestricted NNUE Stockfish 19 (1.25%).
12. **Promotion Adjudication (Phase 14):** Formal adjudication of **Category B — Engineering Progress, Strength Inconclusive**. Baselines X7 and X12 remain unbreached.

### Phase 3: Bad-Capture Deferral Forensics
- Forensic replay of Game 12 from P1.14 Match 2.
- Isolated the root cause of slower-time divergence: Black faced $Qg7\#$ and possessed defensive sacrifice moves $Q\times f6$ ($\text{SEE} = -565$) and $N\times e5$ ($\text{SEE} = -220$). - Proved zero measurable classification overhead (single integer comparison on pre-calculated SEE value).
- Re-tested Game 12: **C1 found the defense and reached depth 8 in only 1,796 nodes ($18.7\times$ fewer nodes than C0's 33,550 nodes)**.

1. Weak pawn structure scoring (isolated, backward, and doubled pawns evaluated naively).

3. Quiescence search tactical omissions in complex exchange chains.
