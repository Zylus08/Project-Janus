## 1. Executive Summary & Statutory Charter Compliance

Milestone P1.17 was chartered to resolve the central experimental contradiction introduced by Milestone P1.16:
*How could candidate Janus Classical X21 score below qualified baselines X7 (42.5%) and X12 (43.5%), yet simultaneously achieve 22.5%–27.5% (29 wins) against world-class Stockfish versions (Stockfish 4, Stockfish 11, and Stockfish 19 NNUE) that earlier Janus engines had never defeated?*

Over the course of 16 rigorous, mathematically controlled phases, Milestone P1.17 answered every question, restored complete tournament integrity, resolved the Correctness–Strength Paradox, and delivered a tactically sound classical engine:

1. **Discovery of Fatal Tournament Harness Defect (100% Attributed):** Discovered that lines 293–300 of `analysis/phase1/p1.16/tests/run_p116_tournaments.py` evaluated resignation adjudication via `termination = "white_win" if score > 0 else "black_win"`. Under the standard UCI protocol, `score cp` is relative to the **side to move**, NOT White. When Black (Janus) was losing by 15 pawns (`score = -1500`), the harness evaluated `score > 0` as False and awarded the win to Black (Janus)! In Match 7 Game 25, Stockfish 19 checkmated Janus on move 55 (`Rh3#`), but because Stockfish reported mate in 1 (`score mate 1`), the harness assigned a win to Janus. 25 of the 29 reported wins were inverted resignations where Stockfish crushed Janus. Re-adjudication and independent replication proved that Janus classical engines score **0.0% to 1.5%** against Stockfish, with **zero real wins**. The P1.16 external results were formally adjudicated as **INVALID**.
2. **Quiescence Correctness Provenance:** Audited 30 tactical positions across X7, X12, X19, X20, and X21. Proved that legacy Q0 quiescence search takes illegal stand-pat beta cutoffs when the king is in check and evaluates leaf checkmates as static positional scores (e.g. Fool's Mate checkmate scored as $-84$ cp). Corrected Q1 quiescence search enforces legal check evasions and evaluates leaf checkmates as true mate scores ($-29,000$ cp).
3. **The Correctness–Strength Paradox Resolved:** Isolated the 0.50-ply search tree reduction caused by searching legal evasions at leaf nodes. Proved that Q1 does legitimate chess work that prevents catastrophic tactical blunders, but in P1.16 exposed primitive classical evaluation flaws (specifically, pieces occupying pseudo-outposts that were easily driven away by enemy pawns).
4. **Targeted Classical Evaluation Intervention (True Outposts):** Implemented True Outpost Evaluation with Enemy Pawn Immunity in `eval_mobility_and_coordination`. Bonuses are awarded exclusively to knights on ranks 4–6 defended by friendly pawns where no enemy pawn on adjacent files can ever attack the square (+28 mg / +18 eg), while transient outposts receive minor bonuses (+8 mg / +4 eg). Bishop central outposts were also integrated (+22 mg / +14 eg). Color symmetry was verified at 100.0% on mirrored FENs.
5. **Candidate Freeze (Janus Classical X22):** Sealed candidate binaries and C++17 source in `analysis/phase1/p1.17/control/` (Executable SHA-256: `35D095CFFE8A2EDA412171803C09BE52A74DFB3F9744C04F573D2A7FFB1C2F1C`).
