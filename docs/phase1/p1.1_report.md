## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone P1.1 represents the first major algorithmic advancement of the **Janus Classical Engine Supremacy Program**. Following the P1.0 audit—which revealed that 84.3% of search CPU time was consumed by un-staged move generation, 74.8% of nodes were consumed by unpruned quiescence expansions, and the effective branching factor was an unsustainable $b^* = 6.56$—Milestone P1.1 modernized Janus Classical X's search pipeline without altering handcrafted evaluation parameters or introducing machine learning dependencies.

Five distinct classical search mechanisms were designed, implemented, and systematically evaluated via isolated ablations (Variants `X1` through `X5`) and an integrated candidate (Variant `X6`):
1. **Staged Move Generation & On-Demand MovePicker (`X1`):** Decouples capture and quiet generation, testing TT moves and killer moves first. Delivers a **3.18x search speedup** and increases raw NPS from 1.28M to 4.27M.
2. **Quiescence SEE Filtering (`X2`):** Prunes negative-SEE captures ($SEE < 0$), eliminating **34.3% of search nodes** with 100% tactical move fidelity.
3. **Null-Move Pruning with Zugzwang Protection (`X3`):** Conservative $R = 3$ reduction conditioned on non-pawn material checks and mate bounds. Slashes search nodes by **61.6%** with zero zugzwang regressions.
4. **Reverse Futility Pruning (`X4`):** Shallow pruning ($d \le 3$, margin $= 80 \times d$) cutting **38.8% of search nodes** with 96.0% move agreement.
5. **Aspiration Windows (`X5`):** Adaptive $[\text{score} - 25\text{ cp}, \text{score} + 25\text{ cp}]$ root window with exponential widening. Cuts nodes by **13.3%** with 100% PV identity.

### Master Experimental Findings:
- **Full Synergy (Variant `X6` vs Baseline `X0`):** Total nodes at depth 7 collapsed from **16.34 million to 2.56 million (84.36% node reduction)**; execution duration plunged from **14.23s to 1.35s (10.52x overall speedup)**; and Effective Branching Factor ($b^*$) plummeted from **6.773 to 5.196**.
- **Search Horizon Expansion:** Under realistic 250 ms game time constraints, average search depth expanded from **6.08 plies to 9.44 plies (+3.36 plies deeper)**.
- **Perft & Correctness:** 100% bit-exact ground-truth pass across all 6 canonical positions (depths 1 to 5) at up to **23.4 million NPS**, with bit-exact parity preserved between C1 and Variant X0.
- **Head-to-Head Playing Strength:** In a 100-game paired blitz match ($1.0\text{s} + 50\text{ms}$) across 50 opening lines, Janus Classical X defeated Classical C1 **87.0 — 13.0 (87.0%)**, scoring **77W - 20D - 3L**, establishing an empirical performance advantage of **+330.2 Elo (95% CI: [+230.4, +430.1] Elo)**.
- **Stockfish 19 Progress Check:** Against unrestricted Stockfish 19 (default NNUE), Janus Classical X broke the historical 0.0% loss floor, securing **3 draws** (7.5% score) and extending average game longevity from 51.2 plies to 64.8 plies.

## 3. Staged Move Generation & Selection (Phase 1)

In Classical C1, `native_generate_legal_moves()` generated all pseudo-legal moves and checked legality via `is_square_attacked()` on every move upfront. Because 78–80% of alpha-beta nodes produce a beta cutoff on the first move, checking legality for subsequent moves was wasted effort.

### Architectural Formulation:
In `engine/classical_x/janus_classical_x.cpp`, a stateful `MovePicker` was constructed:
$$\text{STAGE\_TT} \longrightarrow \text{STAGE\_CAPTURES} \longrightarrow \text{STAGE\_KILLERS} \longrightarrow \text{STAGE\_QUIETS} \longrightarrow \text{STAGE\_DONE}$$

1. **TT Move Selection:** Probed and verified on demand via `is_pseudo_legal_move()`. If valid, searched immediately without move generation.
2. **Captures Generation:** `native_generate_captures()` generates only captures and promotions, ordered via MVV-LVA.
3. **Killer Moves:** Slot 1 and Slot 2 killers verified on demand.
4. **Quiets Generation:** `native_generate_quiets()` invoked **only if all captures and killers fail to cause a cutoff**.

### Qualification Results:
- **Partition Completeness:** 50/50 test positions verified ($\text{Legal} = \text{Caps} \cup \text{Quiets}$, $\text{Caps} \cap \text{Quiets} = \emptyset$).
- **Edge Cases:** 5/5 passed (pinned pieces, double check, underpromotions, en passant discovery safety, castling transit checks).
- **Speedup:** **2.63x to 3.18x faster search execution**, maintaining an 80.4% first-move cutoff rate.
- Full Report: [`analysis/phase1/p1.1/STAGED_MOVE_GENERATION_REPORT.md`](file:///E:/ChessXML/project-janus-phase1/analysis/phase1/p1.1/STAGED_MOVE_GENERATION_REPORT.md).

## 4. Quiescence SEE Filtering (Phase 2)

Quiescence search in C1 previously searched all captures without verifying whether the capture was profitable, causing severe search tree bloat in complex middlegames (74.8% of all search nodes).

### Architectural Formulation:
Static Exchange Evaluation (`native_see()`) was integrated into quiescence search:
$$\text{If } \text{move\_flag}(m) \ne 3 \land \neg\text{in\_check} \land \text{SEE}(b, m) < 0 \implies \text{Prune Capture}$$

### Ablation Results (25 Positions, Depth 7):
- **Q0 (No SEE Pruning):** 16,342,285 nodes (14.07s).
- **Q2 (Conservative $SEE < 0$):** **10,739,035 nodes (-34.3% node reduction, 1.49x speedup)**.
- **Pruning Volume:** **2,806,776 losing captures pruned**.
- **Tactical Fidelity:** **10/10 matches (100.0%)** on tactical test positions.
- Full Report: [`analysis/phase1/p1.1/QUIESCENCE_SEE_ABLATION.md`](file:///E:/ChessXML/project-janus-phase1/analysis/phase1/p1.1/QUIESCENCE_SEE_ABLATION.md).

## 5. Null-Move Pruning (Phase 3)

Null-Move Pruning (NMP) executes a reduced-depth search ($R=3$) after passing the turn. If the score still exceeds $\beta$, a cutoff is returned.

### Preconditions & Guardrails:
$$\text{depth} \ge 3 \land \text{ply} > 0 \land \neg\text{in\_check} \land |\beta| < \text{MATE\_SCORE} - 100 \land \text{NonPawnMaterial}(\text{side}) > 0$$

### Ablation Results:
- **Node Count:** Reduced from 16,342,285 to **6,279,449 nodes (-61.58%)**.
- **Execution Time:** Reduced from 14.12s to **5.20s (2.72x speedup)**.
- **NMP Cutoff Success Rate:** 15,268 cutoffs out of 22,791 null-move probes (**67.0%**).
- **Zugzwang Safety:** Pure pawn endgames (Trebushov, Fine 70) verified with **0 null-move probes**.
- Full Report: [`analysis/phase1/p1.1/NULL_MOVE_PRUNING_REPORT.md`](file:///E:/ChessXML/project-janus-phase1/analysis/phase1/p1.1/NULL_MOVE_PRUNING_REPORT.md).

## 6. Reverse Futility Pruning (Phase 4)

Reverse Futility Pruning (Static Null-Move Pruning) prunes shallow nodes ($d \le 3$) when static evaluation exceeds $\beta$ by a linear depth margin:
$$\text{StaticEval} - (80 \times d) \ge \beta \implies \text{Cutoff}$$

### Ablation Results:
- **Node Count:** Reduced from 16,342,285 to **9,993,931 nodes (-38.85%)**.
- **Execution Time:** Reduced from 13.75s to **8.35s (1.65x speedup)**.
- **RFP Cutoffs:** 814,833 cutoffs out of 1,127,406 probes (**72.3% success rate**).
- **Move Agreement:** **24 / 25 positions (96.0%)** with full alpha-beta search.

## 7. Aspiration Windows (Phase 5)

Aspiration Windows narrow the search window at iterative deepening root plies ($d \ge 5$) to $[\text{prev\_score} - 25\text{ cp}, \text{prev\_score} + 25\text{ cp}]$, exponentially doubling the window on fail-low or fail-high.

### Ablation Results:
- **Node Count:** Reduced from 16,342,285 to **14,169,939 nodes (-13.29%)**.
- **Execution Time:** Reduced from 14.19s to **11.71s (1.21x speedup)**.
- **Re-Search Overhead:** Only **8 widening events** across all 25 benchmark positions.
- **Move Identity:** **25 / 25 positions (100.0%)** exact match.
