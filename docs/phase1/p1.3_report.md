## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone P1.3 delivers the third major engineering advancement of the **Janus Classical Engine Supremacy Program**. Following the integration of continuation history, logarithmic dynamic LMR, and handcrafted positional evaluation in Janus Classical X7 (P1.2), Milestone P1.3 addresses tactical failure modes, shallow horizon blind spots, and external playing strength recovery against unrestricted Stockfish 19 without machine learning.

Five core non-ML search mechanisms were implemented, ablated in isolation, and factorially integrated into **Janus Classical X8**:
1. **Tactical Move Ordering Architecture (Phase 2):**
   - **Capture History ($6 \times 64 \times 6$ tensor):** Tracks historical beta-cutoff conversions for `[attacker][target_sq][victim]`, allowing tactical sacrifice and tension-break captures to supersede static MVV-LVA.
   - **Countermove Heuristic ($64 \times 64$ table):** Contextually orders quiet refutation responses immediately after killer moves.
   - **Empirical Breakthrough:** Elevated tactical puzzle solve rates from 25.0% to **31.25%**, solving the critical central pawn break `TAC_15` (`c4d5`).
2. **Singular Extensions Architecture (Phase 3):**
   - Transposition table (TT) guided singular move identification via reduced-depth verification searches (`se_depth = (depth - 1)/2`) excluding the TT move.
   - Rigorous Transposition Table isolation (`excluded_move == 0` store guard) preventing verification search pollution.
   - S1 conservative margin ($2 \times \text{depth}$) granted 120 singular extensions (26.55% extension rate) across 452 tactical probes.
3. **Multi-Cut Pruning (Phase 4):**
   - Exploits failed singular extension verification subtrees where an alternative move achieves $\ge \beta$ at reduced depth.
   - Pruned **26,257 nodes** (-1.84% tree reduction in tactical positions) across 73 verification cutoffs with zero tactical degradation.
4. **ProbCut Pruning (Phase 5):**
   - Shallow null-window capture searches at `depth >= 5` with conservative margin ($\beta + 200$ cp) pruned 4,059 nodes with 223 early fail-high cutoffs in tactical positions and 179 cutoffs on standard test suites.
5. **Calibrated Dynamic LMR Thresholds (Phase 6):**
   - Replaced the static 10,000 cp barrier with a depth-scaling threshold ($h_{\text{thresh}} = \max(1000, 250 \times \text{depth})$), unlocking dynamic reduction adjustments at shallow and intermediate depths.

## 5. Singular Extensions Architecture (Phase 3)

### Formulation & Verification Safety
When `depth >= 6`, `ply > 0`, `excluded_move == 0`, and `entry.depth >= depth - 3`:
$$s_{\beta} = \text{score}_{\text{TT}} - 2 \times \text{depth}$$
An excluded verification search is executed at $d_{\text{se}} = (d - 1)/2$. If all alternatives fail low below $s_{\beta}$, the move is extended by $+1$ ply. Transposition table stores are strictly prohibited during excluded searches to prevent table corruption.

### Ablation Results
| Mode | Configuration | Extension Margin | SE Probes (Tac) | SE Extended | Extension Rate | Tactical Solved |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **S0** | Disabled (Baseline) | N/A | 0 | 0 | 0.00% | 4 / 16 (25.0%) |
| **S1** | **Conservative SE** | **$2 \times \text{depth}$** | **452** | **120** | **26.55%** | **4 / 16 (25.0%)** |
| **S2** | Aggressive SE | $\text{depth} + 15$ | 451 | 98 | 21.73% | 4 / 16 (25.0%) |

## 6. Multi-Cut & ProbCut Pruning (Phases 4 & 5)

### Multi-Cut Pruning (Phase 4)
- During singular extension verification searches, if an alternative move achieves $\ge \beta$, the entire node fails high immediately.
- **Result:** Pruned **26,257 nodes** (-1.84%) in tactical positions across 73 verification cutoffs with zero loss in solve accuracy.

- Evaluates winning captures with SEE $\ge 0$ at $d-4$ with null window $[-(\beta + 200), -(\beta + 200) + 1]$.
