## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone P1.4 delivers the fourth major engineering audit of the **Janus Classical Engine Supremacy Program**. Convened to address the playing-strength regression discovered in Milestone P1.3, this milestone systematically isolated the fine-grained search mechanisms of Janus Classical X8, uncovered the mathematical mechanism driving its performance deficit, surgically recovered its high-value move ordering features into **Janus Classical X9**, and executed a preregistered, paired-opening strength qualification tournament against the incumbent baseline.

### The Three Master Conclusions

1. **Formal Revocation of the P1.3 Promotion (Category C Determination):**  
   In Milestone P1.3, Candidate X8 was provisionally promoted based on puzzle accuracy and an isolated draw against Stockfish 19, despite losing its head-to-head match against Baseline X7 (+25 =35 -40, 42.50%, $\Delta\text{Elo}: -52.5$, $p = 0.0404$). Independent re-parsing and statistical auditing proved this loss to be statistically significant. Under rigorous scientific protocols, an engine exhibiting a statistically significant playing-strength regression cannot replace the incumbent baseline. **The promotion of X8 is formally revoked.** Janus Classical X7 is officially reaffirmed as the true, unbroken **Qualified Classical Baseline** of Project Janus.

2. **The Nominal Depth Law of Blitz Chess:**  
   Orthogonal feature isolation (B0 through B7) and fixed-time depth profiling proved that **Singular Extensions (SE)** and uncalibrated **ProbCut** were the direct causal drivers of X8's regression. While SE improves puzzle accuracy on sharp tactical positions, its excluded-move verification searches at $(d-1)/2$ consume between 22% and 42% of available search time. Under match clocks (1000ms), this verification tax starved nominal search depth by **1.60 to 1.90 plies** across full games. In typical positional and endgame positions, this depth starvation resulted in fatal tactical horizons.

3. **Surgical Feature Recovery & Candidate X9 Requalification:**  
   Isolating the **Countermove Heuristic ($64 \times 64$ table)** without verification search overhead yielded Candidate X9. Countermoves achieved a **-2.69% reduction in tree nodes** while increasing nominal depth by **+0.30 plies** at 1000ms. In a 200-game qualification match (100 opening pairs at 1.0s + 50ms) against Baseline X7:
   - **Candidate X9 Score:** +67 wins, =60 draws, -73 losses = **97.0 / 200 (48.50%)**.
   - **Rating Differential:** $\Delta\text{Elo}: -10.4$ (Clustered 95% CI: $[-47.6, +26.6]$).
   - **Paired Hypothesis Test:** $t = -0.5600, p = 0.5755$ (statistically indistinguishable from parity).
   - **Relative Recovery:** Recovered **+42.1 Elo** over X8's $-52.5$ Elo regression.

### Formal Promotion Gate Determination

The preregistered promotion gate mandates that a candidate may only replace the incumbent baseline if it demonstrates **statistically significant superiority** ($p < 0.05$, positive $\Delta\text{Elo}$). Because Candidate X9 achieved 48.50% ($p = 0.5755$), it failed to reject the null hypothesis of equal strength.

**Official Determination: Category C — Janus Classical X7 is formally retained as the Qualified Classical Baseline.** Candidate X9 is preserved as an experimental reference demonstrating the upper bound of pure move ordering.

### The Causal Chain of X8 Regression

1. In Candidate X8, when a TT move appears promising, the engine launches an excluded-move verification search at $(d-1)/2$.

3. As a direct consequence, X8 terminated iterative deepening at depth 12 or 13, while Baseline X7 was searching depth 14 and 15.
4. Over a 100-game match, losing 1.9 nominal plies of search depth caused tactical blunders in quiet positions that overwhelmed X8's isolated tactical puzzle gains.
