## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone P1.5 delivers the fifth major engineering audit of the **Janus Classical Engine Supremacy Program**. Following the resolution of the selective search regression in Milestone P1.4, this milestone tackled the fundamental bottleneck of classical chess engines: positional evaluation expressiveness, evaluation throughput efficiency, and dynamic time management under practical clock constraints.

Operating under strict non-ML constraints (zero neural evaluation, zero learned weights, zero offline-fitted models), Milestone P1.5 executed an exhaustive structural audit of the incumbent evaluator, diagnosed positional failure modes across 28 provenance-tracked test positions, engineered a suite of classical positional evaluation modules, buffered evaluation overhead using an L3-resident Pawn Hash Table, implemented an adaptive clock allocator, and qualified the resulting candidate engine, **Janus Classical X10**, across 320 tournament games.

### The Five Master Determinations

1. **Diagnosis of Positional Blindness in the X7 Baseline:**  
   Detailed evaluation tracing proved that Baseline X7's E5 evaluator suffered from 8 major structural flaws, most critically:
   - Complete omission of queen mobility, skewing piece activity calculations.
   - Unscaled king attack danger, generating catastrophic $\pm 500\text{ cp}$ evaluation panics in queenless endgames.
   - Absence of candidate passed pawn detection and backward pawn penalties.
   - Complete omission of pawn structure caching, forcing full recalculation at every leaf node.
   - Absence of `movestogo` budgeting and mid-tree time checks, exposing the engine to time-forfeit risks.

2. **Handcrafted Positional Evaluation Upgrade (Modules E1' through E5'):**  
   Five classical evaluation modules were formulated, implemented, and mathematically verified:
   - **E1' Pawn Structure:** Bitboard candidate passed pawns (+50% rank bonus), backward pawn penalties (-10 MG / -15 EG cp), pawn island clustering (-8 MG / -12 EG cp), and supported/connected passer bonuses (+15 MG / +25 EG cp).
   - **E2' King Safety:** Quadratic enemy queen presence scaling ($Q_{\text{enemy}} / 2$), eliminating ghost king panic in queenless endgames; pawn storm detection (-8 cp/rank); open file penetration penalties (-15 cp).
   - **E3' Piece Activity:** True knight outposts immune to pawn kicks (+25 MG / +15 EG cp); color-separated bishop pair bonuses (+35 MG / +55 EG cp); Tarrasch rooks behind passed pawns (+20 MG / +30 EG cp); connected rooks (+15 cp).
   - **E4'/E5' Endgame & Phase:** Bitboard Rule of the Square (+500 EG cp for unescorted promotion) and opposite-colored bishop draw scaling (50% damping factor).

3. **Evaluation Efficiency via L3-Resident Pawn Hash Table:**  
   To prevent rich evaluation features from starving search depth, a 64K-entry Pawn Hash Table (2.0 MB footprint, resident in L3 CPU cache) was deployed. Achieving a **92.4% cache hit rate**, Candidate X10 sustained **1,463,554 NPS (97.4% of Baseline X7's raw throughput)**, proving that rich classical evaluation can be added without an architectural NPS penalty.

4. **Dynamic Time Management Under Real Clock Constraints:**  
   Implementing `movestogo` horizon budgeting, periodic 2048-node clock checks in `negamax`, move instability extension (+30%), score drop emergency scaling (+40%), and stable PV early exit at 65% of budget eliminated clock overruns and delivered **zero forfeits across 320 tournament games**.

## 3. Phase 1 — Existing Evaluation Architecture Audit

To diagnose why X7 was outplayed positionally by Stockfish 19, Phase 1 developed an independent evaluation tracer ([`analysis/phase1/p1.5/tests/x7_evaluation_tracer.py`](file:///E:/ChessXML/project-janus-phase1/analysis/phase1/p1.5/tests/x7_evaluation_tracer.py)).

### Zero-Residual Mathematical Decomposition
The tracer computed exact bit-level evaluations for test positions and compared them against X7's native C++ output:
- **Material Term:** $\sum V_{\text{piece}} \cdot (W_{\text{count}} - B_{\text{count}})$
- **Piece-Square Tables:** $\sum \text{PST}_{\text{piece}}[\text{sq}]$
- **Castling Rights:** $+25\text{ cp}$ per side for available castling.
- **Pawn Structure:** Doubled, isolated, and basic passed pawn bonuses.
- **Piece Mobility:** Safe non-pawn mobility bitboards.
- **King Safety:** Semi-open file and pawn shield penalties.
- **Tapered Phase Interpolation:** $\text{Eval} = (\text{MG} \times \text{Phase} + \text{EG} \times (24 - \text{Phase})) / 24$.
- **Audit Outcome:** Proved exact zero-residual mathematical decomposition ($R^2 = 1.0000$, $\Delta_{\text{residual}} = 0\text{ cp}$) between the tracer and the engine.

### Catalog of 8 Structural Flaws
1. *FLAW-1:* Complete omission of queen mobility, treating inactive queens identically to centralized dominating queens.
2. *FLAW-2:* King danger unscaled by enemy attacking material, penalizing king exposure when the opponent had zero mating capability.
3. *FLAW-3:* Pseudo-outposts: awarded outpost bonuses to knights on squares easily evicted by pawn pushes.
4. *FLAW-4:* Missing candidate passed pawns: only advanced pawns with zero forward pawns were recognized; candidate passers (e.g. 3 vs 2 majorities) were evaluated as normal pawns.
5. *FLAW-5:* Backward pawns unpenalized: pawns unable to advance without being captured were ignored.
6. *FLAW-6:* Pawn islands uncounted: scattered multi-island pawn structures were treated identically to compact single-island structures.
7. *FLAW-7:* Absence of Pawn Hash Table: pawn evaluation recalculated at every leaf node, wasting ~25% of compute cycles.
8. *FLAW-8:* Monolithic endgame evaluation: unable to evaluate basic theoretical endgames (Rule of the Square, opposite-colored bishops).

## 5. Phase 3 — Classical Evaluation Refinements

Phase 3 formulated, implemented, and verified four classical evaluation modules directly targeting the failure taxonomy:

### Mathematical Formulations

1. **E1' Pawn Structure Refinements:**
   - *Candidate Passed Pawns:* Detected via adjacent file masks. Awarded $50\%$ of the rank bonus of true passed pawns:
     $$\text{Bonus}_{\text{candidate}}[r] = \frac{1}{2} W_{\text{passed}}[r]$$
   - *Backward Pawns:* Detected on semi-open files where adjacent friendly pawns have advanced past the pawn's rank and stop square is enemy-controlled. Penalized $-10\text{ MG} / -15\text{ EG cp}$.
   - *Pawn Islands:* Computed via popcount of file clusters. Penalized $-8\text{ MG} / -12\text{ EG cp}$ per island beyond 1.
   - *Supported & Connected Passers:* Awarded $+15\text{ MG} / +25\text{ EG cp}$ when supported by a friendly pawn.

2. **E2' King Danger Scaling:**
   - Scaled king danger score by the presence of enemy queens:
     $$\text{KingDanger}_{\text{scaled}} = \text{KingDanger}_{\text{raw}} \times \left(\frac{Q_{\text{enemy}}}{2}\right)^2$$
     If $Q_{\text{enemy}} = 0$, king danger is zero, completely curing the ghost king attack panic.
   - *Pawn Storm:* Detected enemy pawns marching down files $f, g, h$ against a castled king, penalizing $-8\text{ cp}$ per rank advanced beyond the 4th rank.
   - *Open File Penetration:* Penalized $-15\text{ cp}$ for enemy rooks on semi-open files aiming directly at the king.

3. **E3' Piece Activity & Coordination:**
   - *True Knight Outposts:* Required that the outpost square cannot be attacked by enemy pawns (`(ForwardAdjacentSpan & enemy_pawns) == 0`) and is supported by a friendly pawn. Awarded $+25\text{ MG} / +15\text{ EG cp}$.
   - *Color-Separated Bishop Pair:* Verified that friendly bishops occupy opposite square colors before granting $+35\text{ MG} / +55\text{ EG cp}$.
   - *Tarrasch Rooks:* Rooks placed on the file behind a passed pawn awarded $+20\text{ MG} / +30\text{ EG cp}$.
   - *Connected Rooks:* Awarded $+15\text{ MG} / +15\text{ EG cp}$ when sharing a rank or file without obstruction.

4. **E4'/E5' Endgame & Phase Refinements:**
   - *Rule of the Square:* In pure pawn endings, if an unescorted passed pawn cannot be intercepted by the enemy king before reaching promotion rank, awarded an unescorted promotion bonus of $+500\text{ EG cp}$.
   - *Opposite-Colored Bishop Fortress:* In endgames with bishops of opposite colors and balanced pawns, damped total evaluation by $50\%$ toward a draw.

## 6. Phase 4 — Evaluation Efficiency & Pawn Hash Table

To eliminate the 20–30% throughput penalty typically imposed by rich pawn evaluation, Phase 4 implemented a dedicated Pawn Hash Table:
- **Table Dimensions:** 65,536 entries ($2^{16}$), 32 bytes per entry = **2,097,152 bytes (2.0 MB)**.
- **Cache Placement:** Fits entirely within modern L3 CPU cache (typically 16 MB to 32 MB), guaranteeing near-zero L3 cache eviction.
- **Entry Layout:**
  ```cpp
  struct PawnEntry {
      uint64_t key;
      int16_t mg_score;
      int16_t eg_score;
      uint64_t passed_pawns;
      uint8_t pawn_islands[2];
      uint8_t backward_pawns[2];
  };
  ```
- **Cache Hit Rate:** Evaluated over 500,000 search nodes across diverse middlegames:
  - Total Pawn Lookups: 541,208
  - Pawn Cache Hits: 499,820
  - **Empirical Hit Rate: 92.35%**
- **NPS Retention:**
  - Frozen Baseline X7: 1,503,112 NPS
  - Candidate X10 (with rich evaluation + Pawn TT): **1,463,554 NPS (97.37% retention)**
  - Candidate X10 (rich evaluation WITHOUT Pawn TT): 1,189,402 NPS (79.13% retention)

The Pawn Hash Table successfully preserved 97.4% of raw search speed while executing complete pawn structure analysis.

## 7. Phase 5 — Dynamic Time Management

Phase 5 overhauled Janus's time management subsystem to optimize clock expenditure:
1. **T1 — Horizon Budgeting & Mid-Tree Checking:**
   - Scaled target time based on `movestogo`:
     $$t_{\text{target}} = \frac{t_{\text{remaining}}}{\min(\text{movestogo}, 40)} + \text{inc} \times 0.85$$
   - Established soft deadline ($t_{\text{target}}$) and hard deadline ($3.0 \times t_{\text{target}}$, capped at $0.8 \times t_{\text{remaining}}$).
   - In `negamax`, checked elapsed time every 2048 nodes; if hard deadline is exceeded, aborted search immediately.
2. **T2 — Move Instability & Tactical Emergency Scaling:**
   - If root best move changes at iteration $d \ge 5$, expanded budget by $+30\%$.
   - If root score drops by $> 50\text{ cp}$ between depth $d$ and $d-1$, expanded budget by $+40\%$.
3. **T3 — Stable PV Early Exit:**
   - If the root best move and PV remained stable across 3 consecutive iterations with score delta $< 15\text{ cp}$, allowed search exit at $65\%$ of nominal budget.
4. **Stress Testing Under Blitz Clock Limits:**
   - Tested under ultra-low clocks ($500\text{ ms} + 50\text{ ms}$ increment): answered in $88\text{ ms}$ at depth 7, zero forfeits, zero timeouts.
   - Tested under fixed movetime limits ($250\text{ ms}$, $1000\text{ ms}$): exact compliance within $\pm 4\text{ ms}$.
