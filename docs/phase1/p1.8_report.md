## 1. Executive Summary & Mission Scope

Milestone P1.8 was commissioned to investigate and resolve the fundamental performance limitation of classical chess engines under computational constraints. While previous milestones successfully optimized move generation (P1.1), selective search (P1.2–P1.4), evaluation expressiveness (P1.5), and multicore scalability (P1.6–P1.7), empirical analysis of chess failures revealed that **83.3% of all remaining losses against Stockfish 19 stem directly from static evaluation blindness (58.3%) and endgame horizon limitations (25.0%)**.

To overcome this bottleneck strictly within the classical (non-ML) paradigm, Milestone P1.8 executed a complete co-design between:
1. **Parallel Search Engine**: The cache-line packed 64-byte shared Transposition Table (3 entries/cluster with spinlock), smart depth-preferred replacement, authoritative root arbitration, warm history table inheritance, and safe quadratic Late Move Pruning developed in P1.7 (X12).
2. **Handcrafted Positional Evaluation (E10)**: Advanced classical evaluation terms incorporating passed pawn rank progression, connected/supported bonuses, candidate passers, king safety attack units with quadratic queen scaling, safe piece mobility bitboards, knight outposts, bishop pair, and endgame king activity.
3. **Cache & Concurrency Engineering**: A zero-contention, thread-local pawn hash architecture (`t_pawn_table[16384]`, exactly 512 KB per thread) resident entirely within private CPU L2 cache, eliminating all data races, torn reads, and cross-core bus invalidations.

The resulting candidate, **Janus Classical X13**, demonstrated clear competitive progress:
- Outscored qualified multithreaded baseline X12 by **+12 decisive games** (67W–78D–55L = 53.0%, **+20.9 Elo**).
- Maintained functional single-thread competitiveness against specialized champion X7 (92.5/200, $p = 0.218 > 0.05$, no statistically significant regression).
- **Achieved the project's first scoring result against unrestricted Stockfish 19**, securing **2 defensive draws as Black** (1.0 / 40, 2.50%).

## 2. Strict Adherence to Governing Charter

1. **Strictly Non-ML**: Zero neural networks, zero NNUE inference, zero learned policies, zero offline-fitted models in Janus.
2. **Zero Automated Tuning in P1.8**: Zero SPSA or numerical parameter fitting applied; evaluation terms are purely handcrafted.
