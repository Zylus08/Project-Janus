Solved the P1.8 "time-control reversal" anomaly by demonstrating that it was purely a small-sample artifact ($N=40$); at 100 games, X14 maintains stable parity ($50.50\%$, $+3.5$ Elo, $p = 0.898$). Because X14 failed to achieve statistically significant superiority over X12 ($48.75\%$, $p = 0.652$) and was outperformed by single-thread champion X7 ($40.25\%$, $p = 0.0014$), Candidate X14 was adjudicated under **Category B (Research Improvement Without Confirmed Strength Advancement)**, preserving X7 and X12 as the official qualified baselines.
