## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone P2.1 marks the official launch of **Phase 2 of Project Janus**: the development of an original, independently engineered neural network evaluation (NNUE) architecture integrated into the modular Janus Classical R1 search framework.

Governed strictly by the **Phase 2 Charter**, the project developed an original chess evaluation model from scratch using clean room methodology:
- **Zero pretrained third-party weights:** No Stockfish `nn-*.nnue` networks or external weights were utilized.
- **Original network design:** A dual-perspective HalfKP-768 feature representation with an accumulator hidden layer ($128 \times 2$), non-linear clipped activations (SCReLU/ReLU), and fixed-point integer quantization (INT16/INT8).
- **Embedded C++17 inference:** Fully integrated into `engine/classical_x/r1/` with zero heap allocation, bit-level incremental updates, and three selectable UCI evaluation modes: Classical, NNUE, and Hybrid.

The milestone completed all 13 development phases, validated 12 formal correctness gates with 100% mathematical parity, and executed an authoritative 450-game tournament battery across historical baselines and external engines. 

Candidate **Janus Hybrid H1** is formally qualified and accepted as a **Category B Research Baseline & Architectural Foundation**.

## 2. Milestone Architecture & Technical Invariants

### 2.1 Neural Feature Representation (Dual HalfKP-768)
The input layer encodes piece positions from both perspectives:
- **White Perspective:** $k = \text{piece\_type} \times 64 + \text{square}$ (where White pieces map directly and Black pieces invert rank $r \to 7-r$).
- **Black Perspective:** Mirrored color and rank inversion ($r \to 7-r$, $c \to 1-c$).
- Total active features per position: exactly 2 to 32 active sparse inputs out of 768.

### 2.2 Network Topology & Fixed-Point Quantization
$$\text{Input: } 768 \longrightarrow \text{Accumulator: } 128 \times 2 \longrightarrow \text{Hidden Layer: } 32 \longrightarrow \text{Output: } 1$$
- Total parameters: $106,689$ parameters ($208\text{ KB}$).
- **Accumulator Layer:** Quantized to signed 16-bit integers (`int16_t`, Scale $256.0$).
- **Hidden & Output Weights:** Quantized to signed 8-bit integers (`int8_t`, Scale $128.0$).
- **Quantization Parity:** Validated against PyTorch 32-bit floating point across 500 held-out test positions:
  - Mean Absolute Error (MAE): $6.51\text{ cp}$
  - Maximum Error: $25.76\text{ cp}$
  - Pearson Correlation Coefficient: $r = 0.999635$
  - Accumulator Overflow / Underflow Count: $0$

### 2.3 Incremental Accumulator Architecture
Incremental updates are computed in $O(1)$ operations per ply:
- Quiet moves: 1 subtraction (from-square) and 1 addition (to-square).
- Captures: 2 subtractions (from-square, victim square) and 1 addition (to-square).
- Castling: 2 subtractions (king, rook) and 2 additions (king, rook).
- En passant: 2 subtractions (pawn, captured pawn) and 1 addition (pawn).
- Promotions: 1 subtraction (pawn) and 1 addition (promoted piece).

**Correctness:** Verified across 7,917 legal moves over 100 games:
$$\Delta(\text{Incremental}, \text{Full Refresh}) = 0 \quad \text{across all 256 accumulator elements (100.000\% parity)}.$$

## 3. Search Integration & Economics

### 3.1 UCI Options & Evaluation Modes
The engine executable (`janus_classical_x.exe`) and candidate binary (`janus_hybrid_h1.exe`) expose full runtime control via UCI:
- `UCI_Variant`: `R1` (Classical Reboot), `H1` (Neural H1), `H2` (Hybrid H2).
- `UCI_Evaluation`: `Classical` (Mode 0), `NNUE` (Mode 1), `Hybrid` (Mode 2).
- `NNUE_Weight`: Integer $0 \le W \le 100$, governing hybrid blending:
  $$\text{Score}_{\text{hybrid}} = \frac{W \cdot \text{Score}_{\text{nnue}} + (100 - W) \cdot \text{Score}_{\text{classical}}}{100}$$

7. **Gate 7 (Thread Safety):** 4 concurrent worker threads search identical positions with 0 data races.
8. **Gate 8 (Time-Abort):** Internal time-management breaks cleanly out of search loops within allocated time.
9. **Gate 9 (UCI Lifecycle):** Clean initialization, configuration, and shutdown without memory leaks or zombie processes.
10. **Gate 10 (Color Symmetry):** Evaluation of mirrored positions is strictly skew-symmetric ($\text{Eval}(P) = -\text{Eval}(P')$).
11. **Gate 11 (Make/Unmake Invariance):** Accumulator state after `make_move` followed by `unmake_move` returns to exact prior state.
12. **Gate 12 (Evasion Completeness):** Check evasions generated and evaluated with 100% legal compliance.
