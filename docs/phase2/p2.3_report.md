## 1. Executive Summary & Charter Compliance

Milestone P2.3 was commissioned to resolve the foundational neural evaluation defects identified in Milestone P2.2:
1. **Feature Space Deficiency:** The lack of explicit king conditioning in DP-PS-768, which left earlier models unable to recognize king safety, pawn cover, or attacking vectors.
2. **Score Compression & Bias:** Severe scale distortion and $+52\text{ cp}$ STM initiative bias caused by uncalibrated outcome-only BCE loss.
3. **Inverted Piece Hierarchy:** Failure to preserve monotonic piece values in opening positions ($Q < N < R$).
4. **Leaf Evaluation Latency:** Repeated full scratch recomputation at search leaf nodes instead of efficient incremental accumulator maintenance.
5. **Competitive Weakness:** Severe Elo deficits on the board against the classical search baseline.

Governed strictly by the **Project Janus Evidence-Driven Research Charter**:
- **Zero Pretrained Weights:** Clean-room implementation; zero access to third-party weights (`nn-*.nnue`).
- **Baseline Anchoring:** Historical controls Classical R1.0 (`7312B2B7...`), H1 (`755C451D...`), H2, X7 (`86392A2F...`), and X22 (`35D095CF...`) remained frozen and unmodified.
- **Evidence-Driven Adjudication:** H3 could not be promoted merely due to larger parameter count, lower validation loss, or theoretical speedup; playing-strength superiority over Classical R1 was the mandatory promotion gate.

Milestone P2.3 successfully designed, trained, quantized, integrated, and evaluated Candidate **Janus Hybrid H3** (HalfKP-40960 NNUE Architecture). Across 18 rigorous phases, H3 established conclusive engineering and scientific advancements in prediction accuracy, feature geometry, and search tree compression. However, under tournament conditions against Classical R1.0, Candidate H3 scored $0.5 / 50$ ($1.00\%$, $-798.3\text{ Elo}$), failing the Primary Strength Promotion Gate.

In accordance with preregistered protocol, Milestone P2.3 is formally adjudicated as:
$$\textbf{CATEGORY B: Neural Engineering & Prediction Advancement}$$
Classical R1.0 is retained as the active, sovereign strength baseline.

## 3. Formal Neural Score Contract (Phase 1)

Milestone P2.3 formally standardized the neural evaluation score contract:
- **Canonical Convention:** Side-To-Move-Relative Centipawns (STM-cp).
- **Scale Mapping:** Sigmoidal logit mapping with scaling temperature $\tau = 400.0\text{ cp}$:
  $$\hat{p} = \sigma\left(\frac{s}{\tau}\right) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-s/\tau}}, \quad s = \tau \cdot \text{logit}(\hat{p}) = \tau \ln\left(\frac{\hat{p}}{1 - \hat{p}}\right)$$
- **Score Bounds:** Clamped to $[-2000\text{ cp}, +2000\text{ cp}]$ for normal play; mate scores reserved for $[\pm 28000, \pm 30000\text{ cp}]$.
- **Perspective Skew-Symmetry:** Verified across 6 invariant transformations:
  $$s(B) = -s(B_{\text{flipped}})$$
  Round-trip floating point numerical precision error was verified at $< 10^{-7}\text{ cp}$.

## 4. HalfKP-40960 Feature Architecture (Phase 2)

Milestone P2.3 implemented genuine king-relative piece encoding:
- **Schema:** 64 King Squares $\times$ 10 Piece Categories $\times$ 64 Piece Squares = **40,960 features**.
- **Piece Categories:**
  - Friendly: Pawn (0), Knight (1), Bishop (2), Rook (3), Queen (4).
  - Enemy: Pawn (5), Knight (6), Bishop (7), Rook (8), Queen (9).
- **Indexing Formula:**
  $$\text{Idx}_{\text{White}}(k_w, c, p, sq) = k_w \times 640 + (c \cdot 5 + p) \times 64 + sq$$
  $$\text{Idx}_{\text{Black}}(k_b, c, p, sq) = (k_b \oplus 56) \times 640 + ((1-c) \cdot 5 + p) \times 64 + (sq \oplus 56)$$
- **Accumulator Oracle Verification:** Verified 100.000% exact incremental accumulator updates against a fresh scratch recompute oracle across $2,217$ legal moves in 12 diagnostic positions and 20 random-walk games with **0 mismatches**.
- **King Refresh Contract:** Friendly king moves trigger full perspective refresh; enemy perspective updates via constant-time $O(1)$ deltas for captured pieces and castling rooks.

## 7. Calibrated Lambda-Loss Design & Ablations (Phase 6)

A 4-way ablation was conducted on identical pilot partitions:
- **L0 (Outcome BCE, $\lambda=0.0$):** Test MAE $311.4\text{ cp}$; severe scale compression and large Startpos bias.
- **L1 (Score MSE, $\lambda=1.0$):** Test MAE $232.8\text{ cp}$; weak endgame win-conversion signals.
- **L2 (Calibrated Mixed, $\lambda=0.8$):** Test MAE **$253.3\text{ cp}$**; optimal balance. Centers Startpos ($+30.3\text{ cp}$) and eliminates uncalibrated STM initiative bias.
- **L3 (Huber Loss, $\delta=200\text{ cp}$):** Test MAE $296.5\text{ cp}$.
$$\mathcal{L} = (1 - \lambda) \cdot \mathcal{L}_{\text{BCE}}(\hat{p}, z) + \lambda \cdot \mathcal{L}_{\text{BCE}}(\hat{p}, \sigma(s_{\text{teacher}}/\tau))$$
