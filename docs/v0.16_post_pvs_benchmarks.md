## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone **v0.16** rigorously validates the committed Principal Variation Search (PVS) implementation under reproducible, controlled benchmarking conditions. The benchmark protocol executes five interleaved paired repetitions across depths 3, 4, and 5, strictly alternating execution order between baseline (PVS disabled) and PVS enabled to eliminate thermal and first-run ordering bias.

### Key Results
- **100% Minimax Invariance:** Across all six benchmark positions at depths 3, 4, and 5, PVS produces **strictly identical root scores, best moves, and legal principal variations** compared to full alpha-beta search (0 mismatches).
- **Consistent Search Tree Reductions:**
  - **Depth 3:** 13,756 $\to$ **13,460 nodes** (**-2.15%**, -296 nodes).
  - **Depth 4:** 67,029 $\to$ **48,308 nodes** (**-27.93%**, -18,721 nodes).
  - **Depth 5:** 211,171 $\to$ **189,620 nodes** (**-10.21%**, -21,551 nodes).
  - In complex middlegame positions such as `open_game`, node count fell by **-32.13%** at Depth 4 (58,282 $\to$ 39,554 nodes) and **-13.25%** at Depth 5 (153,283 $\to$ 132,971 nodes).
- **Statistically Robust Wall-Clock Speedups (AC Mains Connected):**
  - **Depth 4:** Median search time decreased from 4.000s to **2.484s** (**+37.90% median speedup**, **+39.22% mean speedup**), with dispersion (IQR) dropping from 0.376s to **0.172s**.
  - **Depth 5:** Median search time decreased from 13.797s to **11.391s** (**+17.44% median speedup**, **+18.26% mean speedup**).
- **Exceptional Scout Refutation Rate:**
  - Depth 4: 3,403 scout searches $\to$ 12 fail-highs $\to$ 4 full-window re-searches (**99.88% scout refutation rate**; 0.12% re-search rate).
  - Depth 5: 8,071 scout searches $\to$ 22 fail-highs $\to$ 5 full-window re-searches (**99.94% scout refutation rate**; 0.06% re-search rate).

## 2. Experimental Environment & Settings

### Hardware and System Specifications
- **Operating System:** Windows 11 Pro (build 10.0.26200)
- **Host CPU:** 12th Gen Intel(R) Core(TM) i7-12650H (10 cores, 16 threads, base clock 2.30 GHz)
- **Python Version:** 3.12.8 (64-bit)
- **Python Chess Version:** 1.10.0
- **Power Configuration:** AC mains connected and charging (confirmed via `Win32_Battery` CIM interface, `BatteryStatus: 2`).
- **Benchmark Suite:** Six standard positions from `benchmarks/positions.json` (`startpos`, `open_game`, `white_up_rook`, `mate_in_one`, `promotion_tactic`, `stalemate`).

### Protocol & Alternating Order
To eliminate caching, thermal throttling, and process startup bias:
1. Each depth was tested with **5 paired repetitions**.
2. Execution order alternated strictly:
   - **Repetition 1:** Baseline (PVS Disabled) $\to$ PVS Enabled
   - **Repetition 2:** PVS Enabled $\to$ Baseline (PVS Disabled)
   - **Repetition 3:** Baseline (PVS Disabled) $\to$ PVS Enabled
   - **Repetition 4:** PVS Enabled $\to$ Baseline (PVS Disabled)
   - **Repetition 5:** Baseline (PVS Disabled) $\to$ PVS Enabled
3. A 0.5s pause was enforced between paired runs and 1.0s between repetitions.
4. Each run was executed in a fresh, independent Python subprocess (`python scripts/benchmark.py`).

## 6. Correctness and Reliability Audit

1. **Minimax Score & Move Invariance:**
   - In all six benchmark positions across depths 3, 4, and 5, `score_from_side_to_move` and `best_move_uci` match identically between baseline and PVS.
2. **PV Legal Sequenced Execution:**
   - Every move returned in `pv` was validated by playing through the position copy; `pv_legal` was True for 100% of tested runs.
3. **Board State Restoration:**
   - Boards supplied to `engine.search()` are never mutated in-place; FEN string before and after search is identical.
4. **Telemetry Accounting Integrity:**
   - Telemetry satisfies the invariant: $0 \le \text{researches} \le \text{fail\_highs} \le \text{scout\_searches}$.
   - Ordinary alpha-beta cutoffs in baseline mode are tracked cleanly via `beta_cutoffs_disabled` (305 at D3, 2,727 at D4, 6,333 at D5).
