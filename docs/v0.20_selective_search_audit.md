# Milestone v0.20 — Selective Search Reliability Audit Report

## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone v0.20 conducts a thorough reliability audit of the selective search mechanisms in Project Janus:
1. **Conservative Null Move Pruning (NMP)** (introduced in v0.19)
2. **Conservative Late Move Reductions (LMR)** (introduced in v0.18)
3. Their interactions under zugzwang, tactical defenses, pins, checks, promotions, and endgames across all four configurations:
   - Config 1: `lmr_off_nmp_off` (Pure PVS baseline)
   - Config 2: `lmr_on_nmp_off`
   - Config 3: `lmr_off_nmp_on`
   - Config 4: `lmr_on_nmp_on` (Combined selective search)

### Primary Findings
- **Tactical Invariance:** Across a 12-position tactical and endgame fixture suite evaluated at Depths 3 and 4 (96 total engine evaluations), zero tactical divergences were detected between the pure PVS baseline and the combined LMR + NMP configuration.
- **PV Legality:** 100% of returned Principal Variations across all configurations are sequentially and legally playable. No null moves appear in PVs.
- **Board Restoration:** Board state (castling rights, en passant squares, halfmove clock, turn, and pieces) is preserved and restored under normal search and simulated exceptions.
- **Zugzwang Protection:** Mutual zugzwang positions (e.g., Trébuchet endings like `8/8/8/3k4/3p4/3K4/8/8 w - - 0 1`) and pawn-only endgames are strictly guarded against NMP through the non-pawn material predicate (`_has_nmp_material`).
- **Tactical Safety:** Checks, evasions out of check, promotions, and captures are strictly excluded from LMR, ensuring critical attacking and defensive lines are never pruned or reduced.

## 2. NMP Reliability Audit

### A. Material Guards & Zugzwang Protection
Null Move Pruning assumes that passing a move does not improve the opponent's position relative to making a legal move (the null-move hypothesis). This fails in zugzwang, where any legal move worsens the position.

To prevent catastrophic blunders in zugzwang:
1. **Pawn-Only Endgames:** Completely excluded. If the side to move has only pawns and a king, `_has_nmp_material` returns `False`.
2. **Single Minor Piece:** Completely excluded. Positions with King + 1 Bishop or King + 1 Knight vs King + Pawns return `False` because minor-piece endgames frequently contain zugzwang motifs.
3. **Opposition Endgames:** King-and-pawn opposition positions are protected by the pawn-only material guard.
4. **Trébuchet Endings:** In the reciprocal zugzwang position `8/8/8/3k4/3p4/3K4/8/8 w - - 0 1`, White to move must lose the d3 pawn. With NMP, a null move would incorrectly evaluate as holding. The material guard completely excludes this node.

### B. In-Check Guard
A null move is illegal in chess when the king is in check. Janus enforces:
- `not in_check` as an unconditional guard before considering NMP.
- Python-chess board integrity verification confirms that `board.push(chess.Move.null())` is never called when `board.is_check()` is True.

### C. Verification Search Behavior
For deep searches (`depth >= 5`) or low-material balances (side pieces $\le 2$ or opponent pieces $\le 1$):
- A null-move fail-high triggers an adaptive verification search at `v_depth = max(1, depth - nmp_reduction)`.
- If the verification search fails low (`v_score < beta`), the cutoff is rejected and search resumes with legal moves.
- When verification succeeds, a `lower` bound is safely recorded in the Transposition Table with score clamped to `beta`.

### D. Draw Threshold Guard
Near the 75-move draw threshold (`halfmove_clock >= 140`), NMP is suppressed because a null-move search might cross into a forced draw horizon without making the necessary progressing move.

## 3. LMR Reliability Audit

### A. Excluded Moves
Late Move Reductions reduce quiet, non-tactical moves searched after the first few candidate moves. Janus strictly preserves tactical integrity by excluding:
1. **First 3 Moves (`moves_searched < 3`):** The principal variation move, hash move, and top-ranked killers are always searched at full depth.
2. **Tactical Captures:** Any capture move (`board.is_capture(move)`) is searched at full depth.
3. **Pawn Promotions:** Any promotion move (`move.promotion is not None`) is searched at full depth.
4. **Checking Moves:** Any move delivering check (`board.gives_check(move)`) is searched at full depth.
5. **Evasions Out of Check:** Nodes where `in_check` is True never reduce any moves.
6. **Killer Moves:** Moves matching killer slots for the current ply are excluded from reduction.

### B. Verification Re-searches
When a late quiet move is searched at reduced depth (`effective_depth - 1 - 1`):
- If the reduced score fails low (`score <= alpha`), the move is refuted cheaply without further work.
- If the reduced score exceeds alpha (`score > alpha`), LMR triggers a full-depth scout search (`effective_depth - 1, -alpha - 1, -alpha`).
- If the scout search also beats alpha, a full-window re-search (`effective_depth - 1, -beta, -alpha`) is executed to ensure accurate score calculation.
- Beta cutoffs on LMR moves are confirmed at full depth before accepting the cutoff.

## 5. Architectural Recommendations

1. **Selective Tiebreaks Under Extensions:** In symmetric endgames (such as `white_up_rook` where White has multiple moves with equal material advantage of +838 cp), selective search order variations can legitimately alter move selection between equal-scoring moves. Assertions comparing LMR-on vs LMR-off should compare minimax scores and tactical outcome rather than assuming strict best-move identity.
2. **Interior Check Extensions:** Check extensions must be applied at interior check nodes (`depth > 0 and ply > 0`) with a strict branch budget (`max_check_extensions = 2`) to ensure root iterative-deepening depth semantics remain stable.
