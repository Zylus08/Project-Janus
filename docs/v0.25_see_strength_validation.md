### 6. Tactical Observations & Failure Analysis

While SEE ordering accelerates move ordering for favorable exchanges, detailed inspection of games reveals why it does not produce a net playing-strength advantage:
1. **Dynamic Sacrifice Pruning**: In tactical positions, conservative SEE quiescence pruning discards statically negative captures (e.g. sacrificing a rook for a bishop and pawn) that expose the enemy king, allowing the non-SEE engine to find attacks that SEE prunes early.
2. **Move Ordering Convergence**: At depth 3 with MVV-LVA, killer heuristics, and history heuristics already enabled, the principal capture is already ordered near the top of the move list in over 85% of tactical positions. The incremental reordering from SEE does not significantly alter alpha-beta cutoff efficacy.
3. **Endgame Symmetry**: In quiet and technical endgames, both engines reach identical threefold repetition or checkmate trees regardless of SEE.
