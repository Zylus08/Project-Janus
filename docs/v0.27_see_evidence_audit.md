### 1. Executive Summary

Milestone v0.27 conducted a comprehensive, independent audit of the Milestone v0.26 SEE ablation study, its paired match records, move-selection claims, and statistical methodology.

The v0.26 report claimed that Static Exchange Evaluation (SEE) move ordering achieved an exact 50.0% score against the baseline across 100 games at depth 3, reporting zero paired score variance, a confidence interval of `[0.0%, 0.0%]`, and a paired t-test result of `t=0, p=1`. It asserted that the two engines made identical move decisions across all 50 opening pairs, and claimed "exact equivalence."

This milestone audited those findings directly against the raw persisted PGN, CSV, and JSON artifacts. The key conclusions of this audit are:

1. **Move Selection Was Not 100% Identical**:
   - The claim in v0.26 that the engines made identical move decisions in all 50 opening pairs was **empirically false**.
   - Exactly **48 / 50 pairs (96.0%)** produced identical move sequences.
   - Exactly **2 / 50 pairs (4.0%) diverged in move selection**:
     - **Pair 30 (`kings_indian_classical`)**: Diverged at ply 31 (Black). `Janus-SEE-Ordering` played `g2e4` (capturing pawn e4), whereas `Janus-Baseline` played `e6d5` (capturing pawn d5). Both games ultimately concluded in threefold repetition draws.
     - **Pair 45 (`c4_Nf6_english_anglo_indian`)**: Diverged at ply 32 (Black). `Janus-SEE-Ordering` played `e5d4` (capturing pawn d4), whereas `Janus-Baseline` played `d3c1` (capturing on c1). Both games concluded in White checkmating Black.
   - The paired score parity (1.0 – 1.0) was a result of equal game outcomes, not universally identical move choices.
2. **Correction of the Zero-Variance Statistical Analysis**:
   - When every observed paired difference is zero ($\Delta_i = 0$), sample variance is zero and standard error is zero.
   - Under standard mathematical definitions, the Student's paired $t$-statistic is **undefined** ($0/0$ division). Reporting $t=0.0000, p=1.0000$ as a standard inferential test was an arithmetic artifact.
   - A zero-width plug-in bootstrap interval (`[0.0%, 0.0%]`) is degenerate and must **not** be interpreted as proving population equivalence.
   - Using the exact binomial Clopper-Pearson 95% one-sided upper bound, observing $0 / 50$ pairs with score differences only bounds the true population rate of non-tied pairs to $\le 5.82\%$.
3. **Audit of Four-Arm Factorial Benchmarks**:
   - Reconstructed all 360 raw benchmark runs with **0 discrepancies**.
   - Verified that the raw interaction effect $M_D - M_B - M_C + M_A$ is positive for search nodes across depths 3 (+1,464 nodes, +10.9%), 4 (+3,285 nodes, +12.4%), and 5 (+3,465 nodes, +6.5%), confirming sub-additive diminishing returns between capture ordering and quiescence pruning.
4. **Final Implementation Decision**:
   - **Verdict: Category B — No clear strength improvement established.**
   - Retain production defaults with both `enable_see_ordering = False` and `enable_see_pruning = False`.

### 4. Mathematical Correction of the Zero-Variance Statistical Analysis

#### Critique of v0.26 Reporting
In Milestone v0.26, the paired analysis reported:
$$\bar{d} = 0.00\%, \quad \text{SE}_d = 0.00\%, \quad t(49) = 0.0000, \quad p = 1.00000, \quad 95\%\text{ CI} = [0.0\%, 0.0\%]$$

This reporting suffered from critical statistical shortcomings:
1. **Student's $t$-statistic is Undefined**:
   $$t = \frac{\bar{d}}{\text{SE}_d} = \frac{0.0}{0.0} \to \text{Undefined}$$
   The Student's $t$-test assumes a non-zero sample standard deviation. Reporting $t=0, p=1$ masked a division-by-zero fallback in code.
2. **Degenerate Bootstrap Confidence Interval**:
   A plug-in bootstrap resample of identical numbers $\{0, 0, \dots, 0\}$ trivially returns $[0, 0]$. This zero-width interval reflects sample degeneracy, not zero population uncertainty.
3. **Conflating Sample Observation with Population Parameter**:
   Observing $\Delta_i = 0$ across 50 pairs means zero difference was detected *in this sample*. It does not mathematically prove that the population effect size $\mu = 0$.

#### Robust Corrected Formulation
In `scripts/analyze_v027_match.py`, robust statistical handling was implemented:

1. **Zero-Variance Detection**:
   When $s^2 < 10^{-12}$, the Student's $t$-statistic and parametric confidence interval are explicitly marked as `None` (`undefined_zero_variance`).
2. **Exact Binomial Clopper-Pearson Upper Bound**:
   Treating each opening pair as an independent trial where a non-zero score difference could emerge:
   - Observed non-tied pairs: $k = 0$
   - Sample size: $N = 50$ pairs
   - Exact 95% one-sided Clopper-Pearson upper bound on the true rate of non-tied pairs $p_{\text{diff}}$:
     $$p_{\text{upper}} = 1 - \alpha^{1/N} = 1 - 0.05^{1/50} \approx 0.05816 \quad (5.82\%)$$
   *Interpretation*: We cannot rule out with 95% confidence that up to 5.82% of opening pairs in the broader population would exhibit score divergences between the two engines.
3. **Equivalence Testing Requirement**:
   To establish practical equivalence, a pre-declared equivalence margin $\Delta_{\text{bound}}$ (e.g. $\pm 3\%$ or $\pm 5\%$) must be evaluated under a Two One-Sided Tests (TOST) framework. A finite sample showing zero observed variance is consistent with equivalence within bounds $\ge 5.82\%$, but cannot claim "exact equivalence."

### 7. Evaluation of Playing-Strength Match Necessity

In accordance with Section 6 of the mission specification, the necessity of running another playing-strength match was rigorously evaluated:
1. **Redundancy of Identical Depth 3 Testing**:
   Because Project Janus is single-threaded and deterministic for a given position and depth, repeating a 100-game match at depth 3 with the same 50 openings and seed 42 would produce identical games. Re-running identical conditions would provide zero independent statistical information.
2. **Evaluation of Arm C (Pruning-only)**:
   Combined SEE (Arm D, containing pruning) was tested in v0.25 (50.0%), and Ordering-only (Arm B) was tested in v0.26 (50.0%). In both cases, no playing strength advantage was observed. Testing Pruning-only at fixed depth 3 is not justified because the move-level audit established that fixed-depth search trees at depth 3 do not produce quality improvements over MVV-LVA.
3. **Conclusion**: Another 100-game match is **not warranted** for Milestone v0.27. The move-level audit and corrected statistical framework fully resolve the open questions without redundant computation.
