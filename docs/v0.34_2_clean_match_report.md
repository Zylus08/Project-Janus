Every move across all 100 games was independently verified for strict legality from its starting FEN. All PGN, JSON, CSV, manifest, and per-game artifacts reconcile with zero discrepancies.

## 3. Paired Statistical Analysis

Because chess matches with colour-reversed openings exhibit strong pair-level correlation, independent identical distribution (i.i.d.) game assumptions are invalid. We evaluate statistical significance primarily at the opening-pair level ($N=50$ pairs).

### 3.1 Pair Outcomes
- **Pairs Won by Candidate:** 22
- **Pairs Drawn:** 18
- **Pairs Lost to Reference:** 10
- **Total Complete Valid Pairs:** 50 / 50
- **Decisive Pairs:** 32 (22 vs 10, a 2.2 : 1 decisive pair ratio)

### 3.2 Hypothesis Testing & Confidence Intervals

1. **Paired Bootstrap Confidence Interval (10,000 resamples of 50 pairs):**
   - Candidate Score %: **[53.0%, 66.0%]**
   - Elo Difference: **[+20.9 Elo, +115.2 Elo]**
   - *Interpretation:* The entire 95% bootstrap confidence interval lies strictly above 50% (+0 Elo), confirming a statistically robust strength improvement under 500 ms movetime.

2. **Paired t-Test on Pair Score Differentials ($d_i = \text{Score}_i - 1.0$):**
   - Mean differential: $+0.190$ per pair
   - Standard Error: $0.0683$
   - $t$-statistic: **$2.780$**
   - Two-tailed $p$-value: **$p = 0.0054$** ($p < 0.01$, highly statistically significant).

3. **Paired Sign Test on Decisive Pairs ($N=32$):**
   - Two-tailed binomial test: $p = 0.0501$ (borderline significance at $\alpha=0.05$).

## 4. Search and Runtime Diagnostics

| Performance Metric | Candidate (v0.34) | Reference (v0.30) | Delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Average Depth Reached** | 3.18 plies | 3.49 plies | -0.31 plies (-8.9%) |

| **Average Move Time** | 0.5138 s | 0.5115 s | +0.0023 s (+0.4%) |

### Technical Interpretation:
1. **Depth Tradeoff:** Janus v0.34 searches approximately 0.3 plies shallower than v0.30 under the identical 500 ms budget due to the evaluation feature suite (pawn structure, king safety, rook on open files, and phase interpolation).
2. **Positional Guidance:** Despite the slight reduction in raw node throughput and nominal depth, the candidate achieved a **59.5% score (+66.8 Elo)**, demonstrating that the calibrated tapered evaluation terms provide substantially superior positional move selection that outweighs the -0.3 ply search cost.
