### 1. Executive Summary

Milestone v0.34 investigates the central hypothesis emerging from Milestone v0.33: **evaluation function quality in chess engines cannot remain static across distinct game phases; positional terms must smoothly transition between middlegame and endgame dynamics**.

This milestone followed a disciplined 6-stage engineering and validation workflow:
1. **Independent Replication & Audit of v0.33:** Re-verified all raw match artifacts (JSON, CSV, PGN) from v0.33. Verified exact agreement across all 100 games and 50 opening pairs ($52.0\%$ candidate score, $\Delta\text{Elo} = +13.9$, $p \approx 0.46$).
2. **Immutable Evaluation Configuration:** Introduced an immutable, frozen `EvaluationConfig` dataclass, eliminating historical mutable module globals and ensuring strict thread safety across multi-threaded and asynchronous searches.
3. **Phase-Aware Tapered Evaluation:** Implemented classical non-pawn material (NPM) game phase calculation $[0, 24]$ with integer-rounded linear interpolation. Middlegame features (castled king pawn shield, castling rights, semi-open file attacks) smoothly fade out as material trades down, while endgame features (king centralization, amplified passed pawn rank bonuses, intensified doubled/isolated pawn penalties) ramp up.
4. **Dataset Design, Partitioning & Weight Calibration:** Curated 1,100 quiet positions from tournament play across diverse game phases. Enforced strict opening-pair partitioning (Train 646, Val 226, Test 228) to eliminate position leakage. Performed coordinate line search against Texel's logistic win-probability objective.
   - *Key Finding:* In-sample training MSE improved from 0.10512 to 0.10283, but out-of-sample validation MSE degraded from 0.09383 to 0.09484 and test MSE degraded from 0.10387 to 0.10822. Under our strict evidence-based release criteria, the calibrated weights were **rejected** from production defaults due to overfitting, retaining the uncalibrated tapered configuration (`CONFIG_V034_TAPERED`).
5. **Controlled 6-Way Feature Ablation Study:** Evaluated 6 engine configurations across raw evaluation microbenchmarks (50,000 evaluations each) and fixed-depth search benchmarks (depths 4 & 5 across 24 diagnostic positions). Tapered evaluation achieved 43,453 evals/sec in pure Python (only 9.4% overhead over static evaluation) and altered search move decisions on 45.8% of depth 5 positions, demonstrating meaningful phase sensitivity.
6. **Confirmatory Fixed-Time Playing-Strength Match:** Executed a colour-balanced, paired opening match between **Reference Janus v0.30** (`05de411`) and **Candidate Janus v0.34** under fixed **500 ms per move** across all 50 canonical opening pairs (100 games).
   - **Observed Score:** Candidate v0.34 scored **52.5%** (**52.5 / 100 points**: 41 Wins, 23 Draws, 36 Losses).
   - **Pair Record:** **11 Pairs Won, 30 Pairs Drawn, 9 Pairs Lost** (Net +2 pairs won).
   - **Point Estimate:** **$\Delta\text{Elo} = +17.4\text{ Elo}$** ($SE = \pm 34.8$ Elo, 95% CI $[-50.8, +85.6]$).
   - **Significance:** Decisive pairs sign test $p = 0.8238$; paired t-test $t = 0.9272, p = 0.3538$.
   - **Engineering Verdict:** Encouraging positive point estimate (+17.4 Elo, improving on v0.33's +13.9 Elo), but true parity ($0.0\text{ Elo}$) remains inside the 95% confidence interval, classifying the gain as statistically inconclusive under strict release criteria ($p < 0.05$).

### 3. Phase-Aware Tapered Evaluation Architecture

#### 3.1 Game Phase Calculation
The game phase is determined dynamically using standard non-pawn material (NPM) weights:
- Knight: 1
- Bishop: 1
- Rook: 2
- Queen: 4

$$\text{NPM} = \sum (\text{Knights} \times 1 + \text{Bishops} \times 1 + \text{Rooks} \times 2 + \text{Queens} \times 4)$$
$$\text{phase} = \max(0, \min(24, \text{NPM}))$$

- **Starting position:** 4 knights (4) + 4 bishops (4) + 4 rooks (8) + 2 queens (8) = 24.
- **Pawn endgame (bare kings and pawns):** Phase = 0.
- **Monotonicity:** Removing any non-pawn piece strictly decreases or preserves game phase.
- **Symmetry:** Both sides' pieces contribute identically to the global game phase.

#### 3.2 Tapered Score Interpolation
The final evaluation score blends the middlegame score ($S_{\text{MG}}$) and endgame score ($S_{\text{EG}}$) with integer rounding:

$$\text{Score} = \left\lfloor \frac{S_{\text{MG}} \times \text{phase} + S_{\text{EG}} \times (24 - \text{phase}) + 12}{24} \right\rfloor$$

#### 3.3 Phase-Differentiated Positional Terms

| Positional Term | Middlegame Behavior ($\text{phase} \to 24$) | Endgame Behavior ($\text{phase} \to 0$) | Rational Engineering Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **King Centralization** | Inactive ($0\text{ cp}$) | Active ($[-9, +9] \times \frac{14}{12}\text{ cp}$) | In endgames, the king is an attacking piece; in middlegames, centralization is fatal. |
| **Castled Pawn Shield** | $+12\text{ cp}$ per shield pawn | Inactive ($0\text{ cp}$) | Pawn shields protect king from middlegame mating attacks; unnecessary in endgames. |
| **King Open File Penalty** | $-22\text{ cp}$ per open file | Inactive ($0\text{ cp}$) | Open files toward king invite rook/queen attacks in middlegame. |
| **Passed Pawns** | Moderate bonuses ($[0, 5, 10, 20, 35, 60, 100, 0]\text{ cp}$) | Amplified bonuses ($[0, 10, 15, 30, 55, 85, 130, 0]\text{ cp}$) | Passed pawns are decisive promotion threats in simplified endgames. |
| **Doubled Pawns** | $-12\text{ cp}$ penalty | $-16\text{ cp}$ penalty | Doubled pawns are harder to defend and cannot create passers in endgames. |
| **Isolated Pawns** | $-14\text{ cp}$ penalty | $-18\text{ cp}$ penalty | Isolated pawns become chronic weaknesses under endgame piece simplification. |
| **Rook Files** | $+20\text{ cp}$ open, $+10\text{ cp}$ semi-open | $+15\text{ cp}$ open, $+8\text{ cp}$ semi-open | Rook file control remains valuable across all phases. |

#### 3.4 Thread-Safe `EvaluationConfig`
To resolve the architecture flaw in v0.33 where evaluation features relied on mutable module-level global booleans, v0.34 implements an immutable dataclass:

```python
@dataclass(frozen=True)
class EvaluationConfig:
    enable_pawn_structure: bool = False
    enable_rook_files: bool = False
    enable_king_safety: bool = False
    enable_tapered_eval: bool = False
    ...
```

The search engine now receives `eval_config: Optional[EvaluationConfig]` during initialization, completely isolating search instances and preventing cross-thread state pollution.

### 4. Weight Calibration Experiment & Generalization Analysis

#### 4.1 Dataset Design & Partitioning
- **Source:** 100 competitive games from `analysis/v0.33/match/v033_match_500ms.pgn`.
- **Filtering Criteria:**
  - Plies $< 10$ excluded (opening book noise).
  - Plies within 4 of game end excluded (terminal volatility).
  - Positions in check excluded (`board.is_check() == False`).
  - Tactical moves excluded (neither incoming move nor next move is a capture).
  - Sampling: Every 4th quiet ply to eliminate auto-correlation.
- **Total Quiet Samples:** 1,100 positions.
- **Partitioning Strategy (Strict Zero-Leakage):**
  - **Train Partition:** Opening Pairs 1–30 (646 positions, 58.7%)
  - **Validation Partition:** Opening Pairs 31–40 (226 positions, 20.5%)
  - **Held-Out Test Partition:** Opening Pairs 41–50 (228 positions, 20.7%)

#### 4.2 Objective Function (Texel's Tuning)
$$\text{Loss}(\theta) = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N \left( R_i - \frac{1}{1 + 10^{-S(p_i, \theta) / 400}} \right)^2$$
Where $R_i \in \{1.0, 0.5, 0.0\}$ is the game outcome from White's perspective and $S(p_i, \theta)$ is the tapered static evaluation score in centipawns.

#### 4.3 Calibration Results & Overfitting Detection

| Metric | Partition | Uncalibrated Baseline (`CONFIG_V034_TAPERED`) | Calibrated Weights | Delta | Generalization Verdict |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **MSE Loss** | **Train** ($N=646$) | 0.10512 | 0.10283 | **-0.00229** | In-sample improvement |
| **MSE Loss** | **Validation** ($N=226$) | 0.09383 | 0.09484 | **+0.00101** | Out-of-sample degradation |
| **MSE Loss** | **Test** ($N=228$) | 0.10387 | 0.10822 | **+0.00435** | **Out-of-sample degradation** |
| **MAE Loss** | **Train** ($N=646$) | 0.23264 | 0.23084 | -0.00180 | In-sample improvement |
| **MAE Loss** | **Validation** ($N=226$) | 0.23903 | 0.24157 | +0.00254 | Out-of-sample degradation |
| **MAE Loss** | **Test** ($N=228$) | 0.25398 | 0.25931 | +0.00533 | Out-of-sample degradation |
| **Accuracy** | **Test** ($N=228$) | 61.4% | 58.8% | -2.6% | Out-of-sample degradation |

**Engineering Finding:** Coordinate tuning on quiet positions without search tree context caused classic empirical overfitting: in-sample error decreased by 2.2%, but out-of-sample test error increased by 4.2% and predictive accuracy fell from 61.4% to 58.8%.
**Decision:** Under our evidence-based protocol, the calibrated weights were **rejected**. The uncalibrated tapered configuration (`CONFIG_V034_TAPERED`) was selected as the candidate engine.
