## 3. Disclosed Experimental Limitations

1. **Combined Policy Configuration:**
   The adaptive policy combines three parameter adjustments relative to the reference policy:
   - Increment utilization ($W_{\text{inc}} = 0.85$ vs $0.00$)
   - Safety buffer ($R_{\text{safe}} = 50\text{ ms}$ vs $0\text{ ms}$)
   - Horizon divisor ($N_{\text{mtg}} = 30$ vs $40$ moves)
   Consequently, the observed $+107.5$ Elo gain reflects the aggregate system design rather than increment weighting alone.
2. **Time Control Scope:**
   The experiment was conducted exclusively at $3000\text{ ms} + 100\text{ ms}$. Extrapolation to long classical controls (e.g., $90\text{m} + 30\text{s}$) should be validated in future benchmarking before making unconstrained claims.
