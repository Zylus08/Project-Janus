## 3. Audit Findings on Potential Anomalies

### Observation: Operating System Sleep Artifact
During inspection of move telemetry, five moves across games 74–85 exhibited wall-clock durations exceeding 5 seconds (with maximum recorded elapsed time of ~8,197 seconds). 

The auditor confirmed that this was caused by the host operating system entering temporary sleep/standby mode during background execution, during which `time.monotonic()` advanced by the sleep interval while the engine process was paused. 

- Zero engine crashes or timeouts occurred.
- When the host system resumed, the process resumed search, evaluated its normal nodes (~380–1400 nodes), and emitted a legal `bestmove`.
- Because the median duration is an order-statistic robust to extreme positive outliers, the reported median durations (94 ms for Arm A, 109 ms for Arm B, 141 ms for Arm C) are completely unaffected.
- This observation is documented as an environmental observation and does not compromise the technical validity of the match outcomes.
