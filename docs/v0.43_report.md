## 6. Search Telemetry & Failure Attribution (Phase H)

Telemetry across 32,826 game plies identified the causal mechanism:

| Metric | Baseline K0 | Candidate RFP | Delta |
|:---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Total Nodes Searched** | 24,170,174 | 22,660,460 | **-6.25% nodes** |
| **Mean Completed Depth** | 2.438 | 2.559 | +0.12 plies |
| **Mean Move Duration** | 536.3 ms | 152.6 ms | -71.5% move time |
| **Mean Search NPS** | 20,072.5 | 19,324.3 | -3.7% NPS |

### Why Did RFP Underperform as Black?
1. **Initiative Asymmetry:** As White, Janus dictates play; static evaluations are stable, resulting in identical scores (64.5% vs 64.5%).
2. **Tactical Gambits:** In sharp defenses (Evans Gambit, King's Gambit, Tarrasch), Black's static evaluation can appear superficially satisfactory while overlooking tactical piece sacrifices or mating attacks. RFP cutoffs at depth 1 and 2 pruned these lines, causing Black to blunder where Baseline K0 searched deeper tactical lines.
3. **Small-Sample Inflation in v0.42:** The $+29.0\%$ gain in v0.42 was an artifact of sampling noise on 25 non-tactical openings. In an unconstrained 100-opening master suite, RFP performs at parity with Baseline K0.
