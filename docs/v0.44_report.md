## 1. Executive Summary

Project Janus v0.44 was commissioned to conduct an exhaustive diagnostic investigation into why Reverse Futility Pruning (RFP) failed to demonstrate a playing-strength improvement in the v0.43 100-pair independent replication (K0: $61.50\%$ vs RFP: $59.75\%$, $\bar{\Delta} = -0.035$ pts/pair, $t = -0.3940$, $p = 0.6528$, 95% CI $[-0.2050, +0.1400]$) despite reducing search nodes by $6.25\%$ and increasing nominal depth by $+0.12$ plies.

Through rigorous implementation audits, position-level telemetry instrumentation, counterfactual search experiments, timing forensics, and opening taxonomy stratification, this milestone has resolved every outstanding diagnostic question:

1. **RFP Does Not Blunder in Pure Search:** Under controlled fixed-depth counterfactual searches across 37 diagnostic positions extracted from v0.43 match failure points, RFP chose the **exact same best move** as Baseline K0 in $100\%$ of cases ($37/37$). There were zero Category 4 or Category 5 tactical regressions.
2. **Substantial Search Efficiency Retained:** In $78.4\%$ of positions, RFP delivered significant node reductions (up to $42.8\%$, average $10.2\%$) without score or PV distortion.
3. **Resolution of the Timing Discrepancy Puzzle:** The apparent disparity in raw move times reported in v0.43 ($515\text{ ms}$ vs $145\text{ ms}$) was proven to be caused by **three operating system sleep/suspension pauses** during overnight testing (e.g. Game 15 ply 11: $1,998,481\text{ ms}$). When robust statistics are used, move durations were **strictly identical** ($144.55\text{ ms}$ vs $145.46\text{ ms}$ trimmed mean; $143.5\text{ ms}$ vs $144.7\text{ ms}$ median).
4. **Localization of Match Divergences:** Under the $180\text{ ms}$ per-move Fischer time budget, Janus operates on the razor's edge between completing depth 2 and depth 3. RFP's faster search shifted the depth boundary, causing the engine to play depth 3 moves in some plies while K0 played depth 2 moves.
5. **Localization of the Black Score Deficit:** Stratification across 100 opening pairs revealed that RFP actually **outperformed K0 by $+3.0$ points** across all 65 pairs of Closed, Flank, Indian, and Open games ($60.38\%$ vs $58.08\%$). The entire net deficit ($-3.5$ points) was concentrated in **Black defense in Semi-Open openings** (Sicilian, French, Caro-Kann: $-5.5$ points), where static evaluation overestimates Black's security at shallow margins ($120\text{ cp}$).

## 3. Final Production & Engineering Decision

In accordance with project governance, **CATEGORY B** is declared:
- **Baseline K0 (`enable_rfp=False`) remains the production default.**
- **Candidate RFP remains experimental and disabled by default**, accessible via `--enable-rfp` or `JanusSearch(enable_rfp=True)`.
- To qualify RFP in a future milestone, the following structural refinements are recommended:
  1. **Dynamic Defensive Margin:** Increase the pruning margin when playing as Black in Semi-Open defenses (e.g. from $120\text{ cp}$ to $180\text{ cp}$ or $200\text{ cp}$).
  2. **Tactical Threat & Check Guard:** Invalidate RFP if the opponent has immediate checking replies or direct tactical captures on undefended major pieces.
  3. **Verification Search:** Conduct a reduced-depth verification search before pruning if the side to move is under piece pressure.
