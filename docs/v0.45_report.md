## 1. Executive Summary

Project Janus v0.45 investigated the fundamental interaction between Reverse Futility Pruning (RFP), iterative deepening, and constrained wall-clock search budgets.

Building upon the v0.43 replication match (where K0 scored $61.50\%$ vs RFP $59.75\%$) and the v0.44 counterfactual analysis, this milestone evaluated whether RFP's observed search efficiency translates into superior decisions under real-world time controls.

### Core Discoveries:
1. **100% Boundary Divergence Attribution:** Across 259 paired fixed-time trials spanning 7 budget tiers ($50\text{ ms}$ to $1,000\text{ ms}$), **100% of observed move divergences were Category A (Iteration-boundary divergences)**. When both configurations completed the same nominal depth, they selected the **identical best move 100% of the time (Category B = 0%)**.
2. **The Effective Depth Exponential Barrier:** While RFP reliably reduces nodes by $9.19\%\text{--}10.60\%$ in fixed-depth search (depths 4–5), this efficiency saves only $\sim 0.05\text{--}0.12$ plies of effective depth. Because chess branching factors are $\approx 3.5\times$, a $10\%$ reduction is insufficient to overcome the exponential threshold required to complete a full additional iteration under tight time controls. Consequently, mean completed depth under fixed time was identical: **$2.02$ plies for RFP vs $2.03$ plies for K0**.
3. **Equal Decision Quality Against External Oracle:** Evaluated against Stockfish 19 at depth 12 across all 37 diagnostic positions, K0 and RFP achieved **identical move agreement ($21.6\%$)** and identical blunder resistance ($0\text{ blunders}$) at the match budget ($180\text{ ms}$).
4. **Reconciliation of v0.44 Subgroup Reporting:** An audit resolved the apparent arithmetic discrepancy in the v0.44 narrative ($+3.0 - 5.5 = -2.5$ vs $-3.5$): the text highlighted the Semi-Open Black deficit ($-5.5\text{ pts}$) while omitting the Semi-Open White deficit ($-1.0\text{ pt}$). When the complete Semi-Open total ($-6.5\text{ pts}$) is added to the non-Semi-Open total ($+3.0\text{ pts}$), the sum is exactly the observed $-3.5\text{ pts}$.
5. **Publication Invariance Proven:** Automated tests verified that interrupted iterations in `JanusSearch` never overwrite completed iteration moves or scores.
