## 5. Statistical Interpretation & Limitations

1. **Baseline Validity:**
   - 50 games at fixed depth 3 establishes a solid, reproducible, and verifiable operational baseline.
   - It confirms that Janus's search, alpha-beta pruning, quiescence search, move generation, and transposition table run stably over thousands of moves without crashing, leaking memory, or returning illegal moves.
2. **Playing Strength & Elo Rating Limitations:**
   - **No Elo Rating Claim:** A score of 15.0% against Stockfish 19 at fixed depth 3 **does not constitute a calibrated Elo estimate**.
   - Stockfish 19 is not an appropriate peer rating benchmark: its NNUE evaluation and C++ search dynamics operate on a fundamentally different plane than a pure-Python hand-crafted evaluation.
   - Fixed depth 3 creates an artificial horizon. While it provides an upper ceiling benchmark, the tactical errors observed at depth 3 (from both engines) would not occur under standard tournament time controls or deeper searches.

## 6. Recommendations for Next Milestones

1. **Maintain the Baseline:** Keep the 50-game results as the formal v0.5 pre-tuning reference baseline.
2. **Opponent Calibration Before Further Match Testing:**
   - For meaningful rating estimation and tuning feedback, test Janus against calibrated peer opponents (e.g., historical Python engines, or Stockfish with `UCI_LimitStrength=True` / calibrated `UCI_Elo` and node caps).
3. **De-horizoning Search Testing:**
   - Pilot a 10–20 game set at depth 4 or 5 on a subset of these opening pairs to evaluate horizon decay before modifying evaluation weights.
