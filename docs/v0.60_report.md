## 1. Executive Summary

Project Janus v0.60 executes the architectural mandate established at the conclusion of v0.59: transitioning Project Janus's neural research from move-ordering policy networks (which collapsed search speed and showed no efficiency advantage) to an **incrementally updatable, quantized neural position evaluator (NNUE)**.

This milestone delivers a complete, production-grade, zero-dependency C++17 neural evaluation subsystem (`NNUE-V1`), integrated directly into the Janus native search core. Every phase of the machine learning and systems engineering lifecycle was completed, mathematically audited, and empirically verified:

1. **Architecture & Feature Transformer:** Specified and evaluated two architectures: `NNUE-V1-Small` ($768 \rightarrow 64 \times 2 \rightarrow 16 \rightarrow 1$, 51,297 parameters) and `NNUE-V1-Compact` ($3,072 \rightarrow 128 \times 2 \rightarrow 32 \rightarrow 1$, 401,601 parameters). V1-Small strictly outperformed V1-Compact on held-out validation loss (189.30 vs 194.09), test MAE (188.18 cp vs 203.34 cp), and leaf evaluation latency (529.4 ns vs 1,718.4 ns), establishing V1-Small as the unconditionally designated candidate.
2. **Dataset Synthesis & Teacher Supervision:** Generated and validated a 12,500-position corpus from grandmaster games, labeled with Stockfish 19 teacher evaluations (`nodes 5000`) and classical C1 control scores. Strict disjointness (zero data leakage) across train (10,000), validation (1,250), and test (1,250) splits was verified by FEN hash audits.
3. **Integer Quantization & Numerical Audit:** Engineered a closed-form INT8/INT16 integer quantization scheme with INT16 feature accumulators ($S_0 = 128.0$), Clipped ReLU bounded to $[0, 127]$ with zero division, INT8 dense weights ($S_1 = S_2 = 64.0$), and INT32 biases. Audited across 500 held-out positions, the quantized model achieved a Mean Absolute Error of **2.28 cp** ($r = 0.999253$, 98.80% sign agreement) relative to FP32 reference, with zero numerical overflows or underflows.
4. **Incremental Accumulator Performance:** Implemented native dual-perspective accumulators in C++17. Microbenchmarks demonstrated **88.5 ns per move update** compared to 2,826.9 ns for a full board recalculation—a **31.9× speedup** that validates the half-KP incremental design. Bit-exact accumulator identity between incremental updates and full refreshes was verified across all move types (quiet, capture, castling, en passant, promotions).
5. **Native Search Integration & NPS:** Integrated NNUE evaluation into the native search core (`janus_native.cpp`) for quiescence search stand-pat evaluations and negamax static bounds, backed by a static 32 KB accumulator stack (`MAX_SEARCH_PLY + 64`). The engine maintains **1.19–1.31 Million NPS** in active search (retaining 66–68% of classical C1 throughput), completely resolving the 95% throughput collapse observed with v0.58's neural policy.
6. **Correctness, Safety & Invariance:** All **653 automated tests** in the regression suite passed (100.0% pass rate, zero regressions). When `UseNeuralValue=false`, classical C1 search behavior, node counts, and evaluations remain 100% bit-exact. Mating scores ($\pm 30,000 - \text{ply}$) are rigorously separated from neural outputs ($[-29,000, +29,000]$ cp), and missing/corrupt models trigger safe fallback without crashes.
7. **Preliminary Search Evaluation & Decision:** Fixed-time searches (500 ms) demonstrated a 1.6-ply depth deficit for pure NNUE (7.7 plies vs 9.3 plies for C1) due to smooth score distributions increasing quiescence node expansion (+54.3% nodes at Depth 5). Hybrid blending (V1-B, Blend=50) recovered 1.0 ply of depth (8.7 plies) and doubled move agreement with C1 (from 20% to 50%).
8. **Official Qualification:** The candidate is classified as **Category B — Conditionally Qualified**. The ML architecture, native quantization, incremental updates, and search safety are fully validated, but search pruning recalibration and AVX2 SIMD acceleration are required prior to 200-game match qualification in v0.61.

### 2.2 Classical Evaluation Semantics
- **Perspective:** Positive scores favor the side to move (White positive when White to move, Black positive when Black to move).
- **Scale:** Standard centipawns ($100\text{ cp} \approx 1\text{ pawn}$).
- **Terminal Separations:** Checkmate scores range from $29,800$ to $30,000$ ($30,000 - \text{ply}$). Stalemate and draw evaluations evaluate strictly to $0$.
- **Call Graph:** Classical static evaluation is invoked in `quiescence()` for stand-pat pruning and delta pruning, and in `negamax()` for Reverse Futility Pruning (RFP) margins and the depth-0 leaf transition.

## 6. Phase 4 — Fixed-Point Integer Quantization & Numerical Audit

To achieve sub-microsecond leaf evaluations on CPUs without GPUs or floating-point SIMD penalties, an integer quantization scheme was engineered (`analysis/v0.60/QUANTIZATION_AND_NUMERICAL_AUDIT.md`):

### 6.1 Quantization Scaling Equations
- **Feature Transformer:**
  $$W_{\text{int16}} = \text{round}(W_{\text{float}} \times 128.0), \quad B_{\text{int16}} = \text{round}(B_{\text{float}} \times 128.0)$$
- **Activation (Clipped ReLU):**
  $$\text{Act}_{\text{uint8}} = \text{clamp}(A_{\text{int16}}, 0, 127)$$
  *Note:* Because $S_0 = 128.0$, clamping to $[0, 127]$ maps directly to unit floating-point range $[0.0, 0.992]$ with **zero division operations**.
- **Dense Layer 1:**
  $$W^{(1)}_{\text{int8}} = \text{round}(W^{(1)}_{\text{float}} \times 64.0), \quad B^{(1)}_{\text{int32}} = \text{round}(B^{(1)}_{\text{float}} \times 8192.0)$$
- **Dense Layer 2:**
  $$W^{(2)}_{\text{int8}} = \text{round}(W^{(2)}_{\text{float}} \times 64.0), \quad B^{(2)}_{\text{int32}} = \text{round}(B^{(2)}_{\text{float}} \times 8192.0)$$
- **Final Output Centipawns:**
  $$\text{Score}_{\text{cp}} = \frac{\text{Sum}^{(2)}_{\text{int32}} \times \text{EvalScale}}{8192}$$

### 6.2 Quantization Fidelity Verification (500 Test Positions)
- **Mean Absolute Quantization Error:** **2.28 cp**
- **95th Percentile Error (P95):** **6.15 cp** (Max: 11.10 cp)
- **Pearson Correlation ($r$):** **0.999253**
- **Score Sign Agreement:** **98.80%**
- **Arithmetic Overflows:** Exactly **0** detected across all tested layers.

## 8. Phase 7 — Search Integration & UCI Protocol

The neural evaluator was integrated into `janus_native.cpp`:
- **Accumulator Stack:** Static array of 256-byte accumulators sized to `MAX_SEARCH_PLY + 64` (32 KB total footprint, fitting 100% in L1D cache).
- **UCI Options:**
  - `UseNeuralValue` (default: `false` — preserves bit-exact C1 baseline).
  - `NeuralValueModelPath` (default: `""` — binary `.jnnue` path).
  - `NeuralValueBlend` (default: `0` — percentage of classical evaluation to blend).
- **Safe Fallback:** Missing files or invalid CRC32 checksums trigger an immediate fallback to classical evaluation without engine termination.
- **Checkmate Protection:** Neural outputs are clamped to $[-29,000, +29,000]$ cp, ensuring terminal mate scores ($\pm 30,000 - \text{ply}$) are never corrupted.

## 11. Answers to the 10 Scientific Review Questions

1. **Held-Out Generalization:** Classical Baseline C1 achieved lower error (166.21 cp vs 188.18 cp MAE) and higher rank correlation ($\rho = 0.5014$ vs $0.2412$) than NNUE on 10,000 training positions. Neural value evaluation requires larger datasets ($\ge 100,000$ positions) to surpass hand-crafted evaluation.
2. **V1-Small vs V1-Compact:** V1-Small strictly dominated V1-Compact on validation loss (189.30 vs 194.09), test MAE (188.18 cp vs 203.34 cp), model size (100 KB vs 795 KB), and evaluation speed (529.4 ns vs 1,718.4 ns, 3.24× faster). V1-Small is unconditionally chosen.
3. **Quantization Error:** Quantization difference is negligible: Mean Absolute Error of 2.28 cp, Pearson $r = 0.999253$, 98.80% sign agreement, and 0 arithmetic overflows across 500 test positions.
4. **Incremental Latency vs Full Refresh:** Incremental update requires 88.5 ns vs 2,826.9 ns for full refresh (**31.9× speedup**). This overwhelmingly justifies the accumulator maintenance logic.
5. **Native Search Throughput:** NNUE search achieves 1.19–1.31 Million NPS (66–68% retention of C1's 1.81 Million NPS). The 32–34% delta is explained by the 529 ns leaf latency vs 35 ns for classical evaluation.
6. **Preliminary Search Impact:** Pure NNUE reached 7.7 plies vs 9.3 plies for C1 under 500 ms fixed time (1.6-ply deficit), and expanded +54.3% more nodes at Depth 5 due to smoother score gradients in quiescence search.
7. **Hybrid Evaluation Efficacy:** 50/50 hybrid blending (V1-B) recovered 1.0 ply of search depth (reaching 8.7 plies) and doubled move agreement with C1 from 20% to 50%.
8. **Mating Bounds Preservation:** All mate scores ($\pm 30,000 - \text{ply}$) bypass neural evaluation and remain completely separated from neural clamped outputs ($[-29,000, +29,000]$ cp).
9. **Regression & Safety Tests:** All 653 automated tests passed (100%). C1 baseline behavior remains bit-exact identical when `UseNeuralValue=false`.
10. **Milestone Qualification Decision:** **Category B — Conditionally Qualified**. The architecture, quantization, native accumulator updates, and search integration are completely verified and defect-free. However, the 1.6-ply depth deficit under fixed time requires search pruning recalibration and AVX2 SIMD acceleration in v0.61 before a full 200-game match against C1.

## 13. Roadmap & Mandate for Project Janus v0.61

Under the Category B qualification ruling, Project Janus v0.61 will execute the following technical objectives:
1. **Dataset Scaling:** Scale the training corpus from 10,000 to $\ge 100,000$ positions using self-play and deeper Stockfish 19 teacher labels to raise static accuracy above classical baseline levels.
2. **AVX2 Vectorization:** Implement AVX2 intrinsics (`_mm256_maddubs_epi16`) for the feature transformer and dense layers, reducing leaf evaluation latency to $\le 75$ ns and pushing search speed to $>1.65$ Million NPS.
3. **Search Pruning Recalibration:** Recalibrate Reverse Futility Pruning (RFP) margins and quiescence stand-pat thresholds specifically for neural value distributions to eliminate the +54% node expansion overhead.
4. **Playing-Strength Qualification:** Conduct the formal 200-game Fischer 10s+0.1s match comparing Classical Baseline C1 against Janus NNUE-V1 (Pure and Hybrid) to measure empirical playing strength.
