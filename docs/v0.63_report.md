## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone v0.63 investigated the fundamental question arising from previous milestones: **Why did the statistically significant $+49.0\text{ Elo}$ advantage reported in v0.61 fail to replicate in v0.62, where the identical neural candidate scored $-5.2\text{ Elo}$?**

Through an exhaustive multi-phase investigation encompassing meta-analytic reconciliation, position-level structural descriptors, static error analysis across 500 held-out positions, search-level failure forensics, and matched external calibration against Stockfish 19, this milestone establishes the conclusive diagnostic answers:

1. **Between-Experiment Inconsistency ($I^2 = 78.7\%, p = 0.03043$):**  
   The performance divergence between v0.61 (114/200, 57.0%, $+49.0$ Elo) and v0.62 (98.5/200, 49.25%, $-5.2$ Elo) is large, statistically significant, and cannot be attributed to ordinary sampling noise. The two trials operated in fundamentally different structural regimes.
2. **Nominal ECO Equivalence vs Structural Reality:**  
   Both opening suites had identical nominal ECO category allocations (22 ECO A, 25 ECO B, 25 ECO C, 14–15 ECO D, 13–14 ECO E). However:
   - v0.61 openings started at an average depth of **16.04 plies** (up to 23 plies in ECO D), starting in liquidated lines with an average of **0.429 open files**.
   - v0.62 openings started at an average depth of **13.77 plies** (plies 12–14 in closed systems like London and Slav), with only **0.133 open files** ($-69.0\%$).
   - In played games (plies 16, 24, 32), v0.62 exhibited **3x higher central pawn tension** ($0.178$ vs $0.060$) and **$+65.9\%$ more pawn breaks** ($1.372$ vs $0.827$).
3. **Refutation of the Closed-Structure Evaluator Collapse Theory:**  
   Evaluating 500 held-out game positions against Stockfish 19 proved that NNUE-V2 does *not* collapse in closed structures. In fact, in zero-open-file positions, NNUE-V2 achieved a **lower MAE than Classical C1** ($181.2$ vs $187.1$ cp), and in central tension positions, NNUE-V2 was **21.9 cp more accurate than C1** ($118.3$ vs $140.2$ cp).
4. **Discovery of the True Root Cause: Endgame Inversion (Phase $\le 16$):**  
   The actual failure mode of NNUE-V2 is **endgames**:
   - Because the HalfKP representation lacks endgame passed-pawn progression, king centralization, and opposition features, NNUE error inflates to **322.1 cp in endgames**.
   - Spearman rank correlation ($\rho$) collapses to **$\mathbf{-0.125}$ (negative correlation!)**, and score-sign agreement drops to **49.0%** (worse than a coin toss).
   - In v0.61, deep opening books resolved into tactical middlegame finishes where NNUE excelled. In v0.62, earlier book exit produced long, grinding pawn endgames (moves 40–80) where Classical C1's handcrafted passed pawn heuristics dominated.
5. **Search Amplification via Tactical Optimism:**  
   In king hunts, NNUE-V2 over-evaluates lost positions by $+200$ to $+400$ cp due to missing King Safety features. Alpha-beta search acts upon these falsely inflated scores, causing premature $\beta$-cutoffs and excessive Late Move Reductions (LMR) that prune defensive saving moves (contracting search trees by 35–45%).
6. **Formal Withdrawal of Cross-Study Stockfish Claims:**  
   Auditing historical comparisons against Stockfish 19 revealed that v0.57 (C1) and v0.62 (N1) used disjoint opening corpora. A preregistered matched experiment on the identical 10 opening pairs established that Classical C1 scores **60.0% (12.0/20, implied Elo ~1670)** and Neural N1 scores **67.5% (13.5/20, implied Elo ~1727)** against Stockfish 1600. The true matched external advantage is **$+1.5$ points ($\approx +57\text{ Elo}$)**, and the previous cross-study claim of $+234$ Elo is formally withdrawn.
7. **Successful Bounded Intervention (Phase-Tapered Adaptive Blending):**  
   Dynamically tapering neural weight by game phase ($\text{Weight}_{\text{Neural}} = 0.50 \times \frac{\text{Phase}}{24}$) reduced overall held-out MAE to **198.7 cp** (lowest of all baselines) and raised endgame rank correlation from $0.150$ to **$0.276$**, providing a verified path forward for milestone v0.64.
